## 一、题型盘点

对 30 篇完整范文逐一判定题型，分布如下：

| 题型 | 篇数 | 占比 |
|---|---|---|
| Opinion（同意与否，含 positive/negative development） | 13 | ~43% |
| Discussion + Opinion（讨论双方观点） | 9 | ~30% |
| Two-part question（一题两问） | 6 | ~20% |
| Problem + Solution（问题/对策，含 explain + how 变体） | 2 | ~7% |

Simon 自己把题型明确归为四类（原文）："Opinion / Discussion + opinion / Problem + solution / 2-part question"，并强调 "I can write any type of essay in 4 paragraphs; I just need to decide what to put in each paragraph."

### 1. Opinion 型（真题 + Simon 的处理）
- *Wild animals have no place in the 21st century, so protecting them is a waste of resources. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* → **完全反对**（completely disagree），两个主体段各给一个反对理由（动物有生存权/保护栖息地对人类本身至关重要）。
- *Foreign visitors should pay more than local visitors for cultural and historical attractions. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* → **完全反对**，先承认对方逻辑（"The argument in favour of higher prices... would be that..."）再用 "However, I believe this to be a very shortsighted view." 反驳。
- *Some people who have been in prison become good citizens later... these are the best people to talk to teenagers about the dangers of committing a crime. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* → **完全同意**，第二段讲为何前科者最有效，第三段逐一否决替代方案（警察/老师/教育片）。
- *In some countries, many more people are choosing to live alone nowadays... Do you think this is a positive or negative development?* → **各打五十大板**（"both positive and negative consequences in equal measure"），好处一段、坏处一段。
- *As well as making money, businesses also have social responsibilities. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* → **完全同意**，但第一段先让步（企业必须先赚钱生存），第二段展开社会责任。

结构特征：4 段固定；开头第二句必须亮明立场；完全同意/反对时两段都为自己辩护，绝不写"另一派认为"。

### 2. Discussion + Opinion 型
- *Some people believe that studying at university or college is the best route to a successful career, while others believe that it is better to get a job straight after school. Discuss both views and give your opinion.* → 站"上大学"一边，且观点出现在三处（"I give my opinion in 3 places: introduction, paragraph 3, conclusion"——Simon 原话点评）。
- *Some people think that museums should be enjoyable places to entertain people, while others believe that the purpose of museums is to educate.* → **骑墙型**："museums can and should be both entertaining and educational."
- *Nowadays animal experiments are widely used... Some people argue that these experiments should be banned..., while others are in favour of them...* → **中间点**："animal testing 只应保留于最重要的医学研究"（"I agree with the banning of animal testing for non-medical products, but... a necessary evil where new drugs... are concerned"）。
- *Several languages are in danger of extinction...* → 先写"浪费钱"观点一段，再以 "Despite the above arguments, I believe..." 转向自己支持保护。
- *Some people think that strict punishments for driving offences are the key to reducing traffic accidents. Others, however, believe that other measures would be more effective...* → **两者并用**："both punishments and a range of other measures can be used together"。

结构特征：双方篇幅要差不多（"you should write a similar amount for each view"）；问题若要观点，不需额外段落，在开头和结尾亮明即可。

### 3. Two-part 型
- *There are many different types of music in the world today. Why do we need music? Is the traditional music of a country more important than the international music...?* → 一段答"为什么需要音乐"，一段答"传统音乐 vs 国际音乐"。
- *Happiness is considered very important in life. Why is it difficult to define? What factors are important in achieving happiness?* → 一段"为何难定义"，一段"幸福的先决条件（基本生存需求→亲友分享）"。
- *Many people prefer to watch foreign films rather than locally produced films. Why could this be? Should governments give more financial support to local film industries?* → 一段原因、一段"应该资助"（含新西兰《指环王》旅游例）。
- *These days more fathers stay at home and take care of their children while mothers go out to work. What could be the reasons for this? Do you think it is a positive or a negative development?* → 一段原因（平权运动+生活成本），两段正反后结论"positive"。
- *More and more people are migrating to cities... Explain some of the difficulties of living in a city. How can governments make urban life better for everyone?* → 一段城市问题、一段政府对策（实际是 problem/solution 的两问变体）。

结构特征："Just answer the first question in paragraph 2, and the second question in paragraph 3"，两问篇幅相等。

### 4. Problem + Solution 型
- *In the developed world, average life expectancy is increasing. What problems will this cause for individuals and society? Suggest some measures...* → 第 2 段问题（养老金/税基/医疗/照护），第 3 段对策（延迟退休 65→70、鼓励移民、预算转移）。
- *More and more people are migrating to cities in search of a better life, but city life can be extremely difficult. Explain some of the difficulties... How can governments make urban life better...?* → 问题段（生活成本/治安/空气/拥挤）+ 对策段（保障房、限车+公交、发展省城乡镇）。

结构特征：开头不给具体问题和对策——"In the second sentence you don't need to give any causes or solutions; save your ideas for the main paragraphs."

## 二、开头段（Introduction）规律

### 2.1 总原则：两句话，40 词左右
Simon 的原话规则：
> "As I've said before, task 2 introductions should be short and direct. You only need to write two sentences in order to do two things: 1. Introduce the topic. 2. Respond to the question, making your position clear."

配套三条规则（原文）："Write 2 sentences: introduce the topic, then give a general answer. / Mention everything that the question mentions. / Don't save any surprises for the conclusion."

他还给时间预算："5 minutes. Write your introduction: 2 sentences are enough." 以及 "The introduction is necessary, but the main body paragraphs decide your score."

### 2.2 第一句的真实句式变体（改写题目）
| 句式 | 原文实例 |
|---|---|
| **It is true that + 改写** | "It is true that foreign films are more popular in many countries than domestically produced films." |
| **It is sometimes/often argued that + 改写** | "It is sometimes argued that tourists from overseas should be charged more than local residents to visit important sites and monuments." |
| **It is no doubt true that...** | "It is no doubt true that the majority of people would like to be happy in their lives." |
| **People have different views about...**（5 次） | "People have different views about whether we are more or less dependent on others nowadays." |
| **Some people argue/believe that + 对方观点** | "Some people argue that it is pointless to spend money on the protection of wild animals because we humans have no need for them." |
| **In recent years it has become...**（背景+趋势） | "In recent years it has become far more normal for people to live alone, particularly in large cities in the developed world." |
| **直接给定义/事实** | "When they finish school, teenagers face the dilemma of whether to get a job or continue their education." |

### 2.3 第二句的真实句式变体（亮明立场）
| 立场 | 原文实例 |
|---|---|
| 完全反对 | "I completely disagree with this idea." / "I completely disagree with this point of view." |
| 带理由的反对 | "Personally, I disagree with the idea that money is the key consideration when deciding on a career, because I believe that other factors are equally important." |
| 完全同意 | "I completely agree with the idea that businesses should do more for society than simply make money." |
| 让步式部分同意（while 句） | "While I tend towards the viewpoint that animal testing is morally wrong, I would have to support a limited amount of animal experimentation..." |
| although 句 | "Although it can be argued that governments could save money by allowing this to happen, I believe that these languages should be protected and preserved." |
| 各半 | "In my opinion, this trend could have both positive and negative consequences in equal measure." |
| 回答两问 | "There could be several reasons why this is the case, and I believe that governments should promote local film-making by subsidising the industry." |
| 问题/对策型通用句 | "There are several causes of this alarming trend, but measures could certainly be taken to tackle the problem." |

他还总结过一个可直接套用的 **'while' 公式**（原文）：
> "While I accept argument A, I favour argument B"

### 2.4 十个真实开头段（原句摘录）
1. "Some people argue that it is pointless to spend money on the protection of wild animals because we humans have no need for them. I completely disagree with this point of view."（题目原词几乎全换：no place→pointless；waste of resources→spend money）
2. "It is true that foreign films are more popular in many countries than domestically produced films. There could be several reasons why this is the case, and I believe that governments should promote local film-making by subsidising the industry."
3. "People have different views about whether we are more or less dependent on others nowadays. In my view, modern life forces us to be more independent than people were in the past."
4. "It is sometimes argued that tourists from overseas should be charged more than local residents to visit important sites and monuments. I completely disagree with this idea."
5. "When they finish school, teenagers face the dilemma of whether to get a job or continue their education. While there are some benefits to getting a job straight after school, I would argue that it is better to go to college or university."
6. "It is true that new technologies have had an influence on communication between people. Technology has affected relationships in various ways, and in my opinion there are both positive and negative effects."
7. "In recent years it has become far more normal for people to live alone, particularly in large cities in the developed world. In my opinion, this trend could have both positive and negative consequences in equal measure."
8. "It is true that people in industrialised nations can expect to live longer than ever before. Although there will undoubtedly be some negative consequences of this trend, societies can take steps to mitigate these potential problems."
9. "Traffic and pollution are growing problems in today's society. Personally, I disagree with the idea that higher petrol prices could solve these problems, and I believe that various other measures would be more constructive."（一并回答了 "What other measures...?" 这半个问题）
10. "It is true that weddings have become more costly and extravagant in recent years. There are several reasons for this, and in my view it is a negative trend."（Simon 专门演示的 "really succinct introduction"）

### 2.5 改写题目的具体手法（同义替换）
- **词性/结构转换**："average life expectancy is increasing" → "people in industrialised nations can expect to live longer than ever before"（名词短语→动词句）；"More houses are needed" → "new housing is therefore needed" / "increase the provision of housing"。
- **近义词替换**：bigger and more expensive weddings → "more costly and extravagant"；security measures are being increased → "security... has been tightened"；a waste of resources → "pointless to spend money"。
- **上下义词具体化**：strict punishments → "small fines, licence suspension, driver awareness courses, and even prison sentences"。
- **保留必要的题目关键词**：Simon 自己点评 security 一题时说 "I've used some of the words from the question, but there are some nice changes too (e.g. tightened, feel restrictive)" ——不追求每个词都换。
- **反对的题目用对方观点开头**："Some people argue that it is pointless to..."，第二句再反击。

## 三、主体段（Main Body）规律

### 3.1 "一个观点 = 一个段落"
Simon 全文通常只有 **2 个主体观点**（每段 1 个），原话：
> "This means that we need 2 main body paragraphs (2 main ideas)."

以及他对自己方法的量化描述：
> "my 250-word essays usually contain 13 to 15 sentences: 2 for the introduction, 5 to 6 for each main paragraph and 1 sentence for the conclusion."

### 3.2 段内两种固定写法（原话）
> "For main paragraphs, I teach them two easy ways to organise their ideas:
> 1. **Idea, explain, example**: If you have one main idea, this easy format will help you to build a good paragraph.
> 2. **Firstly, secondly, finally**: If you have two or three ideas, just use this format. Miss the 'finally' if you only have two ideas..."

变体提示（原文）："A variation on this format is 'idea, example, explain'." 以及一篇作文内**不要两段都用 firstly/secondly**："it's best not to use the same structure twice in one essay."

### 3.3 段落长度与主题句
- 目标：**5 句、85–110 词**（prisons 段 85 词/5 句；plants-and-animals 段 99 词；life-expectancy 段 106 词；money-work 段 116 词——均为原文标注）。
- 主题句写法（原文）："A good way to start a paragraph is with a short, simple sentence that introduces the main idea of the paragraph." 一个观点时直接亮观点（"In my opinion, junk food is the main cause of childhood obesity."）；两三个观点时不点破，用 "There are two main causes of..."。
- 主体段占全文约 70%（原文）："The main paragraphs make up around 70% of the essay. These paragraphs are the key to a high score."

### 3.4 完整真实主体段逐句标注

**段落 A（idea → explain 递进 → 真实例子，85 词，Simon 本人分析过）**
> ① On the one hand, criminals do need to be punished in some way. ② A person who commits a crime must learn that unlawful actions have consequences. ③ Prison sentences punish offenders because they lose their freedom, and are separated from family and friends. ④ In this way, prison acts as a deterrent to make people think carefully before breaking the law. ⑤ Last year in the UK, many people were given a prison sentence for rioting, and hopefully this punishment will deter them from similar behaviour in future.

- ① 主题句（短、简单）
- ② 解释：犯罪有后果（总）
- ③ 解释：自由的剥夺即惩罚（具体化）
- ④ 解释：推导出威慑作用（结果）
- ⑤ 真实例子（英国骚乱判刑）
Simon 的原句点评："The paragraph contains 5 sentences, with a total of 85 words... Sentences 2, 3 and 4 develop the main idea in a logical, step-by-step way: crime has consequences - loss of freedom is the punishment - this stops people from breaking the law. Sentence 5 contains a real example."

**段落 B（firstly/secondly 结构，电影话题）**
> ① There are various reasons why many people find foreign films more enjoyable than the films produced in their own countries. ② Firstly, the established film industries in certain countries have huge budgets for action, special effects and to shoot scenes in spectacular locations. ③ Hollywood blockbusters like 'Avatar' or the James Bond films are examples of such productions and the global appeal that they have. ④ Another reason why these big-budget films are so successful is that they often star the most famous actors and actresses, and they are made by the most accomplished producers and directors. ⑤ The poor quality, low-budget filmmaking in many countries suffers in comparison.

- ① 主题句（不点破观点数量："various reasons"）
- ② 理由一（firstly）
- ③ 例子（具体片名）
- ④ 理由二（用 "Another reason" 替代 secondly）
- ⑤ 对比收束（回扣本土电影）

**段落 C（example 驱动型，hobbies 话题，7 句）**
> ① On the one hand, many people enjoy easy hobbies. ② One example of an activity that is easy for most people is swimming. ③ This hobby requires very little equipment, it is simple to learn, and it is inexpensive. ④ I remember learning to swim at my local swimming pool when I was a child, and it never felt like a demanding or challenging experience. ⑤ Another hobby that I find easy and fun is photography. ⑥ In my opinion, anyone can take interesting pictures without knowing too much about the technicalities of operating a camera. ⑦ Despite being straightforward, taking photos is a satisfying activity.

- ① 主题句 → ② 例 1 引入 → ③ 例 1 解释（三连并列短句）→ ④ 例 1 个人经验 → ⑤ 例 2 引入 → ⑥ 例 2 解释 → ⑦ 小结句回扣"easy 但 enjoyable"。
Simon 注："Notice that we used examples as the basis of both main paragraphs."

**段落 D（坏处段，video games 话题）**
> ① However, I would argue that these benefits are outweighed by the drawbacks. ② Gaming can be highly addictive because users are constantly given scores, new targets and frequent rewards to keep them playing. ③ Many children now spend hours each day trying to progress through the levels of a game or to get a higher score than their friends. ④ This type of addiction can have effects ranging from lack of sleep to problems at school, when homework is sacrificed for a few more hours on the computer or console. ⑤ The rise in obesity in recent years has also been linked in part to the sedentary lifestyle and lack of exercise that often accompany gaming addiction.

- ① 段内转折主题句（回扣上段）→ ② 机制解释（because 从句）→ ③ 现状具体化 → ④ 后果推进（ranging from...to...）→ ⑤ 追加后果（also + 被动 linked to）。

### 3.5 例子怎么写
- 一律 1–2 句、可验证："'Avatar' or the James Bond films"、"Messi or Ronaldo"、"In Liverpool, for example, there are several new statues and sculptures in the docks area"、"In London, for example, the introduction of a congestion charge"、"Last year in the UK... prison sentence for rioting"、个人经历例 "I remember learning to swim..."（一句话带过）。
- 引出方式："For example,"（24 次）/"For instance,"（1 次）/"such as"（8 次）/"To take the UK as an example" / "To use a common example of this practice" / "A good example of this situation is London"。
- Simon 的建议："If you can't think of any good ideas, start by thinking of some examples"——例子反过来生成观点。

## 四、结尾段（Conclusion）规律

### 4.1 规则
- **一句话、绝不引入新观点**（原话）："The conclusion is very short, and simply summarises what I had already written. Never put any new ideas in the conclusion." / "You don't need to say anything new; just paraphrase what you wrote in your introduction or summarise your overall answer."
- 换词重述立场即可，"loosely paraphrasing the introduction"（他对 sports salaries 一文的自注："I paraphrased the overall idea, rather than word for word"）。
- 时间预算："Last 5 minutes. Write a quick conclusion then check your work."

### 4.2 他给的分题型结论模板（原文）
1. Opinion: "For the reasons mentioned above, I believe that... (+ repeat your opinion)."
2. Discussion (+ Opinion): "In conclusion, there are convincing arguments both for and against... (topic), but I believe that..."
3. Advantages and Disadvantages: "In conclusion, I would argue that the benefits of... outweigh the drawbacks."
4. Problem and Solution: "In conclusion, it is clear that there are various reasons for... (topic), and steps need to be taken to tackle this problem."

### 4.3 五个真实结尾段（原句摘录）
1. "In conclusion, we have no right to decide whether or not wild animals should exist, and I believe that we should do everything we can to protect them."（把开头 "I completely disagree" 具象化为 "no right to decide"）
2. "In conclusion, while salaries certainly affect people's choice of profession, I do not believe that money outweighs all other motivators."（while 让步 + 换词重申：key consideration → outweighs all other motivators）
3. "In conclusion, it may save money in the short term if we allow minority languages to disappear, but in the long term this would have an extremely negative impact on our cultural heritage."（short term / long term 对照，深化而非新增）
4. "For the reasons mentioned above, it seems to me that students are more likely to be successful in their careers if they continue their studies beyond school level."（不用 In conclusion 的变体）
5. "In conclusion, technology has certainly revolutionised communication between people, but not all of the outcomes of this revolution have been positive."（"revolution" 一词呼应正文）

（超短结论示例："In conclusion, I do not believe that any financial concessions should be made for people who choose private education."——17 词，也判 band 9。）

## 五、观点数量规律

- **全文通常只有 2 个观点**（每段 1 个），来源是动笔前的 10 分钟构思："I tell my students to spend around 3 minutes making notes for each main body paragraph."
- **完全同意/反对时**：两段全为自己的立场辩护，不需要提反方（原话）："When you completely agree or completely disagree with the question statement, you don't need to write about the other side of the argument. Just state your opinion and give reasons." 实例：wild animals（两段分别驳"无生存价值"与"浪费资源"两个分论点）、teenagers unpaid work（对青少年无益/对社会无益）、private schools tax、gender and university（不现实/不公平）。
- **部分同意（partly agree）时**：开头用 while/although 句式亮明"两边各取一半"，然后 "On the one hand... On the other hand..." 各写一段（原文结构 B："Introduction: topic + say that you 'partly agree' → On the one hand,... → On the other hand,... → Conclusion: repeat that you accept elements of both arguments"）。实例：hobbies（简单爱好和困难爱好都有乐趣）、salary vs other factors、helping poor countries（本国与国外都该帮）。
- **如何决定立场**：不看真实想法，看哪个好写（原话）："What is the easiest opinion to explain? What good vocabulary could I use?" / "Don't worry about putting your real opinion; just try to think of the easiest opinion for a 4-paragraph essay."
- **讨论型他站哪边**：三种处理都有——
  - 站第二边：university vs job（上大学）、minority languages（保护）、fixed punishments（个案量刑）；
  - 中间点/两边都要：museums（both entertaining and educational）、artists funding（政府+其他来源）、road safety（惩罚+其他措施并举）、animal testing（仅限医学研究）；
  - 篇幅上第一边略多、第二边（自己认同的）同样充分，观点在开头、第 3 段、结尾三处出现。
- **平衡型观点的常见错误**（原话）："The big mistake that students make when trying to give a balanced answer is that they write about what 'some people' and 'other people' think. This question asks for your views, not the views of other people."

## 六、连接词使用规律（数据说话）

对 30 篇完整范文（约 9,100 词）做词频统计：

| 表达 | 次数 | 说明 / 典型语境 |
|---|---|---|
| **In conclusion** | 29 | 每篇恰好 1 次，永远段首 |
| **For example** | 24 | 段首 "For example, ..." 或插入语 "In the UK, for example," |
| **if**（条件句） | 29 | 推演后果："If we destroyed these areas, the costs... would far outweigh..." |
| **I believe** | 29 | 立场标记，全文散布 |
| **while** | 20 | 让步从句："While I accept that..., I believe..." |
| **which**（关系从句） | 21 | 句间衔接："..., which would help to reduce both air pollution and traffic congestion" |
| **also** | 19 | 追加："They could also impose 'green taxes'..." |
| **Firstly** | 12 | 段内第一条理由 |
| **In my opinion** | 15 | 立场标记 |
| **Secondly** | 10 | 段内第二条理由 |
| **However** | 10 | 句首转折（多为段内主题句转折） |
| **On the one hand / On the other hand** | 10 / 10 | 双边型作文的固定段首 |
| **Finally** | 9 | 段内最后一条理由（**Thirdly: 0 次**，从来不用） |
| **therefore** | 9 | 多在句中："governments will **therefore** receive less money in taxes" |
| **such as** | 8 | 举例嵌入 |
| **and this**（指示衔接） | 7 | "..., and this encourages them to learn patience" |
| **At the same time** | 6 | 段首转第二观点："At the same time, I do not believe that society has anything to gain..." |
| **Another + 名词** | 6 | "Another advantage / issue / reason / key factor / hobby..." |
| **In this way** | 4 | 结果推导："In this way, prison acts as a deterrent..." |
| **In other words** | 3 | 同义重申（ 解释→换说法） |
| **As a result** | 3 | 结果句 |
| **Personally / In my view / I would argue** | 5 / 4 / 5 | 立场标记变体 |
| **meaning that / which means that / this means that** | 3+2+2 | 解释性衔接："Penalties... can act as a deterrent, meaning that people avoid repeating the same offence." |

**显著缺席的"高级"连接词**（同一语料中次数）：Moreover **0**、In addition **0**、Additionally **0**、Thus **0**、Consequently **0**、Hence **0**、Not only...but also **0**；Furthermore 仅 **3** 次。Simon 的"禁词令"原文：
> "Moreover - Native speakers hardly ever use this word, and students tend to use it wrongly. Just use 'furthermore' or 'also'. / Demerits - ... Just use 'disadvantages' or 'drawbacks'. / Hence / Thusly - These words sound too formal and old-fashioned. Just use 'therefore' or 'as a result'."

他还反复说（原文）："You can get a band 9 using 'firstly, secondly, finally' if the ideas between these linking words are good."、"Examiners care much more about the content between the linkers."

**比连接词更重要的 5 种隐性衔接**（他自己的清单）："Use this or these to refer to the idea in the previous sentence. / Use pronouns like it and they... / Repeat a key word throughout the paragraph. / Repeat a key idea in different ways. / Develop an idea from 'general' to 'specific'."（实例：artists 段落中 art → artworks → this kind of art 的名词复现与 this/these 指代。）

## 八、句子风格规律

### 8.1 句长（30 篇完整范文，376 句实测）
- 平均 **20.8 词/句**，中位数 21 词；
- **69.7% 的句子落在 15–30 词区间**（典型的"一句一个完整意思"）；
- ≤10 词的短句约 8%（几乎全是主题句、结尾句，如 "I completely disagree with this idea."）；
- ≥35 词的长句约 3%（集中在结尾段和论证主干句）。

### 8.2 全文骨架（Simon 自述 + 实测吻合）
- 全文 250–295 词、**13–15 句**："2 for the introduction, 5 to 6 for each main paragraph and 1 sentence for the conclusion"；
- 段落：**4 段为默认**（个别多问题 5 段：environment、media 两篇）；
- 主体段占全文 70%。

### 8.3 长句构造手法（从范文归纳）
1. **which 非限定从句收尾**（衔接 + 附加信息）："...investing in better public transport, which would mean that fewer people would need to travel by car."
2. **if 条件句推演后果**："If travellers realised that they would have to pay more..., they would perhaps decide not to go to that country on holiday."
3. **while/although 让步主干**："While I accept that these games can sometimes have a positive effect on the user, I believe that they are more likely to have a harmful impact."
4. **分词短语作结果状语**："..., therefore helping to boost that economy too." / "..., meaning that information is presented in limited depth."
5. **并列三连短成分**（最 Simon 式的"伪复杂"）："This hobby requires very little equipment, it is simple to learn, and it is inexpensive."
6. 名词化+介词短语："with regard to personal success in life" / "For a variety of reasons, it would be wrong to..."

他对"难句"的态度（原话）："Notice that I'm not trying to be 'difficult' or 'original' - I'm just writing normal, clear English!"

### 8.4 数字与百分比的用法
只在具体化处出现，从不堆数据："perhaps from 65 to 70"（退休年龄）、"about £8 per day"（伦敦拥堵费）、"if fifty per cent of the places needed to go to males"、"eight or more hours each day"、"it took me around two years"、"there are sometimes hundreds of applicants for one position"。

## 九、中国考生最容易模仿错的点：10 条对照

| # | Simon 做了什么 | 考生常做错什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 开头两句话、约 40 词，第一句改写题目、第二句亮立场 | 背"With the development of society..."式万能背景；他点名批评 memorised 的 "We live in a rapidly changing globalized world" 和陈词滥调 "hotly debated issue"（"Examiners hate phrases (cliches) like..."） |
| 2 | 观点在开头亮明、贯穿全文（引用评分标准："a band 7 essay 'presents a clear position throughout the response'"）；"We don't like 'surprise conclusions'" | 把观点藏到结尾"惊喜揭晓"，或全文态度暧昧 |

| 5 | 得分靠主题词伙和搭配（"topic vocabulary is the key to a high score, not 'difficult' words"） | 堆砌 demerits、thusly 等大词；他原话："'The demerits can be succinctly summarized thusly' looks memorised, unnatural and forced. Nobody writes like this in the real world!" |
| 6 | 一篇只有 2 个观点，每段 5 句 85–110 词，深挖到底（idea → explain → example） | 观点贪多求全，一段塞 3 个点各写半句，段落只有 2-3 句就草草收场 |
| 7 | 每段"一个观点=一个段落"，观点间靠 this/these、代词、关键词复现衔接 | 段内多线并行、靠连接词硬拼；他原话："the ideas that you are trying to link are MUCH more important than the linking itself!" |
