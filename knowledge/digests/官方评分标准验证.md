## 0. 最重要的事实：官方评分标准在 2023 年 5 月改版了

本地 03 文档与 Simon 的解读都基于 **2023 年 5 月之前的旧版 public version**。官方 PDF 现行版首行标注 **"Writing Band Descriptors — Updated May 2023"**，官方新闻页明确说明写作量表经过审阅更新（"In May 2023, following an extensive review process, updated Writing assessment scales... were published"，大意为正式发布更新版量表供下载使用）。

实质要求大多保留，但**多处标志性措辞被修改**——其中恰好包括 Simon 点过的 CC 9 分 "attracts no attention"。因此本地文档**不必推翻，但引用"官方原句"处需要按现行版更新**，并把"出自旧版/新版"标清楚。

## 1. 官方原文（现行版，2023-05）

### 1.1 Task 2 · Task Response

| 分 | 官方原文 |
|---|---|
| 9 | "The prompt is appropriately addressed and **explored in depth**. A clear and **fully developed position** is presented which directly answers the question/s. Ideas are relevant, **fully extended and well supported**. Any lapses in content or support are extremely rare." |
| 8 | "The prompt is appropriately and sufficiently addressed. A clear and **well-developed position** is presented in response to the question/s. Ideas are relevant, **well extended and supported**. There may be occasional omissions or lapses in content." |
| 7 | "**The main parts of the prompt are appropriately addressed.** A clear and developed position is presented. Main ideas are **extended and supported**, but there may be a tendency to over-generalise or there may be a lack of focus and precision in supporting ideas/material." |
| 6 | "The main parts of the prompt are addressed (though some may be more fully covered than others). An appropriate format is used. A position is presented that is directly relevant to the prompt, although the conclusions drawn may be **unclear, unjustified or repetitive**. Main ideas are relevant, but some may be **insufficiently developed** or may lack clarity, while some supporting arguments and evidence may be less relevant or inadequate." |
| 5 | "The main parts of the prompt are incompletely addressed. The format may be inappropriate in places. The writer expresses a position, but the development is not always clear. Some main ideas are put forward, but they are limited and are not sufficiently developed and/or there may be irrelevant detail. There may be some repetition." |

旧版（本地文档所依据的版本）对照要点：7 分旧文为 "addresses **all parts of the task**; presents a clear position **throughout**; presents, extends and supports main ideas..."——现行版把 "all parts" 收窄为 "the **main** parts ... appropriately addressed"，并删去了 "throughout"。

### 1.2 Task 2 · Coherence and Cohesion

| 分 | 官方原文 |
|---|---|
| 9 | "The message can be followed effortlessly. **Cohesion is used in such a way that it very rarely attracts attention.** Paragraphing is skilfully managed." |
| 8 | "The message can be followed with ease. Information and ideas are logically sequenced, and cohesion is well managed. Occasional lapses in coherence and cohesion may occur. Paragraphing is used sufficiently and appropriately." |
| 7 | "Information and ideas are logically organised, and there is **a clear progression throughout the response**. A range of **cohesive devices including reference and substitution** is used flexibly, but with some inaccuracies or some over/under use. Paragraphing is generally used effectively to support overall coherence, and the **sequencing of ideas within a paragraph** is generally logical." |
| 6 | "Information and ideas are generally organised well, and the message generally progresses coherently. Cohesive devices are used to some good effect but cohesion within and/or between sentences may be **faulty or mechanical due to misuse, overuse or omission**. The use of **reference and substitution** may lack flexibility or clarity and result in some repetition or error. Paragraphing may not always be logical and/or the **central topic may not always be clear**." |
| 5 | "Information and ideas are arranged but with limited or no clear overall progression. Cohesive devices may be **inadequate, misused, overused or missing**. Paragraphing may be inadequate or missing." |

对照要点：
- CC 9 旧文 "uses cohesion in such a way that it **attracts no attention**" → 现行 **"very rarely attracts attention"**（官方放宽了一档措辞）。
- CC 7 旧文有 "presents a clear **central topic** within each paragraph"，现行版已删；"central topic" 现在出现在 **6 分的负面项**（"the central topic may not always be clear"）。"每段一个中心"的要求仍然成立，但出处应改为 6 分负面项。
- "progression" 一词在 **CC 栏**（7 分），不在 TR 栏。

### 1.3 Task 2 · Lexical Resource

| 分 | 官方原文 |
|---|---|
| 9 | "A wide range of vocabulary is used accurately and appropriately, with **very natural and sophisticated control** of lexical features. Minor errors in spelling and word formation are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication." |
| 8 | "A wide resource is fluently and flexibly used to convey precise meanings. There is **skilful use of uncommon and/or idiomatic items** when appropriate, despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation." |
| 7 | "The resource is sufficient to allow **some flexibility and precision**. There is **some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items**. An awareness of **style and collocation** is evident, though inappropriacies occur. There are only a few errors in spelling and/or word formation, and they do not detract from overall clarity." |
| 6 | "The resource is generally adequate and appropriate for the task. The meaning is generally clear in spite of a rather restricted range or a lack of precision in word choice. **If the writer is a risk-taker, there will be a wider range of vocabulary used but higher degrees of inaccuracy or inappropriacy.** There are some errors in spelling and/or word formation, but these do not impede communication." |
| 5 | "The resource is limited but minimally adequate for the task. Simple vocabulary may be used accurately but the range does not allow much variation in expression. Frequent mistakes in spelling and/or word formation may be noticeable and cause some difficulty for the reader." |

对照要点：旧版 7 分写的是 "uses **less common lexical items** with some awareness of style and collocation"；现行版改为 "less common **and/or idiomatic** items"。本地文档"less common items & collocations"的实质含义不变，引文需加 "and/or idiomatic"。

### 1.4 Task 2 · Grammatical Range and Accuracy

| 分 | 官方原文 |
|---|---|
| 9 | "A wide range of structures is used with **full flexibility and control**. Punctuation and grammar are used appropriately throughout. Minor errors are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication." |
| 8 | "A wide range of structures is flexibly and accurately used. **The majority of sentences are error-free**, and punctuation is well managed. Occasional, **non-systematic errors** and inappropriacies occur, but have minimal impact on communication." |
| 7 | "A variety of complex structures is used with some flexibility and accuracy. Grammar and punctuation are generally well controlled, and **error-free sentences are frequent**. A few errors in grammar may persist, but these do not impede communication." |
| 6 | "A mix of simple and complex sentence forms is used, but with limited flexibility. Examples of more complex structures are **not marked by the same level of accuracy** as in simple structures. Errors in grammar and punctuation occur, but rarely impede communication." |
| 5 | "The range of structures is limited and rather repetitive. Although complex sentences are attempted, they tend to be faulty, and the greatest frequency of errors occurs in these structures. However, the errors do not often impede communication." |

对照要点：9 分旧文 "full flexibility and **accuracy**; rare minor errors occur only as 'slips'" → 现行 "full flexibility and **control**"。7 分官方标尺句 "error-free sentences are frequent" 与 8 分 "the majority of sentences are error-free" 的分档梯度，是 GRA 判 7 还是 8 的关键。

### 1.5 Task 1 与 Task 2 的差异点（官方原文）

- T1 第一项叫 **Task Achievement**（T2 叫 Task Response）。现行版 Academic/GT 用括注区分，如 9 分 "All the requirements of the task are fully and appropriately satisfied."；8 分 "covers all the requirements of the task appropriately, relevantly and sufficiently. Key features are clearly presented, highlighted and illustrated."
- **overview 是 7 分硬门槛（Academic）**：TA 7 "It presents a **clear overview**, the data are appropriately categorised, and main trends or differences are identified."；TA 6 只要求 "A **relevant overview is attempted**. Information is appropriately selected and supported using data/figures." → 由此可推断"无清晰 overview 则 TA 到不了 7"（官方无此字面句，属合理推断）。
- **数据要 selective**：TA 6 明文 "Information is appropriately selected and supported using figures/data"；负面项含 "Some irrelevant, inappropriate or inaccurate information may occur in areas of detail"（细节层面允错，但关键特征须覆盖）。
- CC/LR/GRA 三栏 T1 与 T2 几乎相同，仅把 prompt 换成 task/需求表述；T2 独有的"position/立场"表述在 T1 中不存在。

## 2. 与本地 03 文档的逐条比对结果

### 2.1 验证为准确（可放心保留）

| 本地文档内容 | 官方依据 | 结论 |
|---|---|---|
| 四项各占 25%、任务分=四项平均 | 官方："The criteria are weighted equally and the score on the task is the average."（ielts.org 评分说明） | **准确** |
| 短板效应强（6.75→7.0 可以，某项掉到 5.5 就只剩 6.5） | PDF 卷首规则："A script must **fully fit the positive features** of the descriptor at a particular level. **Bolded negative features will limit a rating**."（保守给分+负面限档） | **准确，且找到了官方机制出处** |
| TR 的 extend/develop 链条（5 罗列→6 泛泛→7 有展开→8/9 充分展开） | "insufficiently developed"(6) / "extended and supported"(7) / "well extended and supported"(8) / "fully extended and well supported"(9) | **准确** |
| CC 6 机械衔接、指代不清 | "faulty or mechanical due to misuse, overuse or omission... reference and substitution may lack flexibility or clarity" | **准确** |
| CC 7 衔接多样（含指代）、少用机械连接 | "A range of cohesive devices including reference and substitution is used flexibly" | **准确** |
| CC 9"衔接察觉不到、行云流水"（概念） | 现行 "very rarely attracts attention" + "The message can be followed effortlessly" | **概念准确，引文需更新**（见 2.2） |
| LR 7=不常见词伙+搭配意识 | "some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items. An awareness of style and collocation is evident" | **准确**（引文加 and/or idiomatic） |
| LR 9 "natural and sophisticated" | "with **very natural and sophisticated control** of lexical features" | **逐字确认，未过时** |
| 连接词不算词汇分、别堆大词 | LR 各档均评词汇/搭配本身；"skilful use of uncommon and/or idiomatic items when appropriate" | **与官方口径一致** |
| GRA 7=复杂句频繁无误、错误少且小 | "**error-free sentences are frequent**. A few errors... do not impede communication" | **准确** |
| GRA 8=多数句无误、偶发（非系统性）小错 | "majority of sentences are error-free... Occasional, **non-systematic** errors" | **准确**（本地"系统性错误"对应官方 non-systematic，术语可对齐） |
| GRA 6=简单句+复杂句混合、复杂句错误多 | "more complex structures are not marked by the same level of accuracy as in simple structures" | **准确** |
| T1 无 overview 则 TA 上限 6 | TA 7 要求 "presents a clear overview"，TA 6 仅 "a relevant overview is attempted" | **推断成立，建议标注"系由官方分档推得，非官方原句"** |
| T1 数据 selective、只挑关键数据 | "Information is appropriately selected and supported using data/figures" | **准确** |
| 字数不足会罚分（T2 ≥250） | 官方格式页："You must write at least 250 words and **will be penalised if your answer is too short**." | **方向准确**；具体扣几分官方未公布（见 3.2） |
| 总分出现 .5、四舍五入 | 官方：Overall Band Score = 四科平均，"rounded to the nearest half band"；官方惯例 .25 进到 .5、.75 进到整档（只进不退） | **准确**（写作单科内部同理按半档计） |

### 2.2 需要修正/更新（按重要度）

1. **CC 9 引文过时（必改）**。本地："uses cohesion in such a way that it **attracts no attention**"。现行官方："Cohesion is used in such a way that it **very rarely attracts attention**."（2023-05 改版放宽）。教学含义不变，但作为"官方原句"引用必须更新，否则与现行 PDF 对不上。
2. **TR 7 引文过时（必改）**。本地"回应题目的**所有部分**；立场**全程**清晰一致"对应旧版 "addresses all parts of the task; presents a clear position throughout"。现行官方："**The main parts of the prompt are appropriately addressed. A clear and developed position is presented.**"——"all parts"→"main parts appropriately addressed"，"throughout"一词已删。立场一致性仍是备考要求，但引文应标版本。
3. **TR 6 措辞不准（小改）**。本地"结论可能不清楚/**不一致**"；官方为 "conclusions drawn may be **unclear, unjustified or repetitive**"（不清晰、**缺乏依据**或**重复**）。
4. **TR 8 "结论自然"无官方出处（小改）**。官方 8 分要点是 "A clear and well-developed position... Ideas are relevant, well extended and supported. There may be occasional omissions or lapses in content."（允许偶有遗漏）。建议改为"立场清晰且充分展开；允许偶有内容疏漏"。
5. **TR 9 可补强**。本地"观点高度相关、充分展开、有细节"对应 "well supported"；现行 9 分还有 "appropriately addressed and **explored in depth**"（深入探讨），建议补进，这是 8/9 的分水岭表述之一。
6. **"每段一个中心"的出处变了（微调）**。旧版 CC 7 有 "presents a clear central topic within each paragraph"，现行版删除；"central topic" 移到 **CC 6 负面项**（"the central topic may not always be clear"）。自查清单第 2 条（每段一个中心/主题句）继续可用，但应注明其官方落点是"6 分负面项+段落有效性"。
7. **Simon 的 "extend / develop / progress" 三动词归位（微调）**。extend、develop 确在 TR 栏（fully developed position / extended and supported / development）；**progress（progression）在 CC 7 栏**（"a clear progression throughout the response"），不在 TR 栏。本地把三个动词都归到"TR 栏的官方钥匙"，需把 progression 挪到 CC。
8. **GRA 9 用词更新（微调）**。旧 "full flexibility and **accuracy**"→现行 "full flexibility and **control**"；并新增 "Minor errors are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication."
9. **TR 5 补充**。现行 5 分首句是 "The main parts of the prompt are **incompletely addressed**"，本地"只部分回应题目"与此一致，可补英文原句。

## 3. 补充的官方细节（本地文档没有的）

### 3.1 Task 2 权重是 Task 1 的两倍（战略级遗漏）
官方格式页原句："**Task 2 contributes twice as much as Task 1 to the Writing score.**"（另见官方评分说明 "The assessment of Task 2 carries more weight in marking than Task 1."）。即写作总分 ≈ (T1 + 2×T2)/3。本地第 1 节只讲了四项各 25%，漏掉了 T2 占 2/3 这条最划算的提分事实，建议补入"考官怎么打分"一节。

### 3.2 字数规则的官方口径全貌
- 官方明文（ielts.org 格式页）："You must write at least 250 words and **will be penalised if your answer is too short**."；同时 "**you will not be penalised for writing more than 250 words**"（但过长会挤占检查时间）。
- 评分表内嵌的长度规则：**"Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1."**（四项通用，band 1 原文）；band 3 有 "The resource is inadequate (which may be due to the response being significantly underlength)"；GRA band 3 有 "Length may be insufficient to provide evidence of control of sentence forms"。
- **官方从未公布"少 N 词扣 M 分"的公式**。"不足 250 直接封顶 6 分/扣 1 分"等说法是考官经验与培训机构归纳，引用时应标注"半官方/经验规则"。
- 其他官方罚则（同页）：跑题（off-topic）扣分；不以连贯成文书写（用 notes/bullet points）扣分；**抄袭将 severely penalised**；抄抄题目原句不计入字数与评分（评分表卷首 "**Any copied rubric must be discounted**"）。

### 3.3 保守给分原则（支撑本地"短板效应"）
PDF 卷首规则："A script must **fully fit the positive features of the descriptor at that level**. Bolded text indicates **negative features that will limit a rating**." —— 即"够不着该档正面特征就不给该档、踩中负面特征就压档"。这为本地"逐项过清单、按最低项保守估计"的自评流程提供了官方机制依据。

### 3.4 6 分 LR 的 "risk-taker" 条款（鼓励冒险用词的官方依据）
LR 6 原文："**If the writer is a risk-taker, there will be a wider range of vocabulary used but higher degrees of inaccuracy or inappropriacy.**" —— 官方明确承认"敢用更大词汇范围→允许更高错误率"，可直接支持本地"别只写安全小词"的教学主张。

### 3.5 总分计算的权威表述汇总
- 官方："Each task is assessed independently. **The criteria are weighted equally and the score on the task is the average.**"（每项任务独立评，任务分=四项平均）
- 官方："**The assessment of Task 2 carries more weight in marking than Task 1**"（+格式页 "twice as much"）
- 官方（总分离散化）："The Overall Band Score is the average... **rounded to the nearest half band**"；官方惯例 **.25→进到 .5，.75→进到整档（只进不退）**。本地示例 "TR7 CC7 LR6 GRA7 = 6.75 → 7.0" 与官方只进不退惯例一致，示例可保留。

## 4. 总体结论

本地 03 文档的**框架与教学要点全部经得起对照**（四项等权、TR 展开链条、CC 机械衔接/指代、LR less common 词伙+搭配、GRA error-free sentences 分档、T1 overview 逻辑、字数罚分方向），且 "natural and sophisticated"、"error-free sentences are frequent"、"non-systematic" 等关键表述逐字或高度吻合。

需要动手的只有两类：
1. **更新 3 处过时引文**（CC 9 "attracts no attention"→"very rarely attracts attention"；TR 7 "all parts/throughout"→"main parts appropriately addressed"；LR 7 "less common lexical items"→"less common and/or idiomatic items"），并全文注明"引文以 2023-05 版为准"；
2. **补入官方细节**：T2 双倍权重、保守给分/负面限档规则、copied rubric 与抄袭罚则、20 词以下直接 Band 1、"risk-taker" 条款、TR 6 "unjustified or repetitive" 的准确措辞。
