## 剑桥雅思10（Academic + General Training）

### 剑桥10 Test 1
- **Task 1 类型**: pie charts（双饼图：澳大利亚家庭能源使用 + 由此产生的温室气体排放）
- **Task 1 题干**: The first chart below shows how energy is used in an average Australian household. The second chart shows the greenhouse gas emissions which result from this energy use. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 左图 Australian household energy use：heating 42%, water heating 30%, other appliances 15%, refrigeration 7%, lighting 4%, cooling 2%；右图 Australian household greenhouse gas emissions（图内数据文本层不清）
- **Task 2 题型**: two-part（agree/disagree + 开放问题）
- **Task 2 题干**: It is important for children to learn the difference between right and wrong at an early age. Punishment is necessary to help them learn this distinction. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion? What sort of punishment should parents and teachers be allowed to use to teach good behaviour to children?

### 剑桥10 Test 2
- **Task 1 类型**: tables（双表格：1999 与 2004 年五个欧洲国家 Fairtrade 认证咖啡与香蕉销售额）
- **Task 1 题干**: The tables below give information about sales of Fairtrade-labelled coffee and bananas in 1999 and 2004 in five European countries. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: Coffee（1999/2004，百万欧元）：UK 1.5/20, Switzerland 3/6, Denmark 1.8/2, Belgium 1/1.7, Sweden 0.8/1；Bananas：Switzerland 15/47, UK 1/5.5, Belgium 0.6/4, Sweden 1.8/1, Denmark 2/0.9
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: Some people think that all university students should study whatever they like. Others believe that they should only be allowed to study subjects that will be useful in the future, such as those related to science and technology. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑桥10 Test 3
- **Task 1 类型**: bar charts（双柱图：2008 年英国本科与硕士毕业生全职工作以外的去向）
- **Task 1 题干**: The charts below show what UK graduate and postgraduate students who did not go into full-time work did after leaving college in 2008. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: Destination of UK graduates (excluding full-time work) 2008：可见数字 29,665 / 17,7351（疑为 17,735，OCR 噪声）/ 3,500，与 Part-time work / Voluntary work / Further study / Unemployment 四类去向的对应关系[原文不清]；Postgraduates 2008 仅可见数字 3,000，其余[原文不清]
- **Task 2 题型**: opinion（positive or negative development 型）
- **Task 2 题干**: Countries are becoming more and more similar because people are able to buy the same products anywhere in the world. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

### 剑桥10 Test 4
- **Task 1 类型**: diagram（流程图：鲑鱼的生命周期）
- **Task 1 题干**: The diagrams below show the life cycle of a species of large fish called the salmon. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 2 题型**: advantage-disadvantage（outweigh 型）
- **Task 2 题干**: Many museums charge for admission while others are free. Do you think the advantages of charging people for admission to museums outweigh the disadvantages?

### 剑桥10 GT Test A（General Training）
- **Task 1 类型**: letter（请求信：写给住在国外的朋友求助找兼职）
- **Task 1 题干**: You are going to another country to study. You would like to do a part-time job while you are studying, so you want to ask a friend who lives there for some help. Write a letter to this friend. In your letter: give details of your study plans; explain why you want to get a part-time job; suggest how your friend could help you find a job. You do NOT need to write any addresses. Begin your letter as follows: Dear............,
- **Task 2 题型**: problem-solution（原因 + 对策）
- **Task 2 题干**: In many countries, the amount of crime is increasing. What do you think are the main causes of crime? How can we deal with those causes?

### 剑桥10 GT Test B（General Training）
- **Task 1 类型**: letter（感谢/表扬信：写给餐厅经理）
- **Task 1 题干**: You and some friends ate a meal at a restaurant to celebrate a special occasion, and you were very pleased with the food and service. Write a letter to the restaurant manager. In your letter: give details of your visit to the restaurant; explain the reason for the celebration; say what was good about the food and the service. You do NOT need to write any addresses. Begin your letter as follows: Dear Sir or Madam,
- **Task 2 题型**: advantage-disadvantage
- **Task 2 题干**: Some parents buy their children a large number of toys to play with. What are the advantages and disadvantages for the child of having a large number of toys?

## 剑桥雅思11（Academic，本书无 GT 套题）

### 剑桥11 Test 1
- **Task 1 类型**: pie charts（六幅饼图：世界六个地区不同用途用水比例）
- **Task 1 题干**: The charts below show the percentage of water used for different purposes in six areas of the world. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 六地区 North America / South America / Europe / Africa / Central Asia / South East Asia；三类用途 Industrial use / Agricultural use / Domestic use（各地百分比散落于文本层，如 48%、19%、10%、53% 等，具体对应关系[原文不清]）
- **Task 2 题型**: opinion（agree/disagree）
- **Task 2 题干**: Governments should spend money on railways rather than roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

### 剑桥11 Test 2
- **Task 1 类型**: pie charts（双饼图：2000 与 2010 年英格兰某大学英国学生额外语言能力比例）
- **Task 1 题干**: The charts below show the proportions of British students at one university in England who were able to speak other languages in addition to English, in 2000 and 2010. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 语言类别 No other language / French only / German only / Spanish only / Another language / Two other languages（各扇区数值文本层不清）
- **Task 2 题型**: opinion（agree/disagree，法律强制类）
- **Task 2 题干**: Some people claim that not enough of the waste from homes is recycled. They say that the only way to increase recycling is for governments to make it a legal requirement. To what extent do you think laws are needed to make people recycle more of their waste?

### 剑桥11 Test 3
- **Task 1 类型**: line graph（曲线图：1967-2007 年英国、瑞典、意大利、葡萄牙人均 CO2 排放量）
- **Task 1 题干**: The graph below shows average carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions per person in the United Kingdom, Sweden, Italy and Portugal between 1967 and 2007. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 四国图例 United Kingdom / Sweden / Italy / Portugal；纵轴约 0-12（metric tonnes），横轴 1967-2007
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: Some people say that the only reason for learning a foreign language is in order to travel to or work in a foreign country. Others say that these are not the only reasons why someone should learn a foreign language. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑桥11 Test 4
- **Task 1 类型**: table + pie charts（表格：翻新前后 Ashdown Museum 参观人数；双饼图：同期访客满意度调查）
- **Task 1 题干**: The table below shows the numbers of visitors to Ashdown Museum during the year before and the year after it was refurbished. The charts show the result of surveys asking visitors how satisfied they were with their visit, during the same two periods. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 参观人数：翻新前 74,000，翻新后 92,000；满意度类别 Very satisfied / Satisfied / Dissatisfied / Very dissatisfied / No response（各占比文本层不清）
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: Many governments think that economic progress is their most important goal. Some people, however, think that other types of progress are equally important for a country. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

## 剑桥雅思12（Academic，套题编号 Test 5-8）

### 剑桥12 Test 5
- **Task 1 类型**: bar chart（柱状图：2010 年澳大利亚不同年龄段男女性规律运动比例）
- **Task 1 题干**: The bar chart below shows the percentage of Australian men and women in different age groups who did regular physical activity in 2010. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 年龄组 15 to 24 / 25 to 34 / 35 to 44 / 45 to 54 / 55 to 64 / 65 and over；Male / Female 两组
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: Some people believe that it is good to share as much information as possible in scientific research, business and the academic world. Others believe that some information is too important or too valuable to be shared freely. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑桥12 Test 6
- **Task 1 类型**: maps（地图题：Islip 小镇中心现状与发展规划）
- **Task 1 题干**: The maps below show the centre of a small town called Islip as it is now, and plans for its development. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 现状：Main road、Shops、Housing、Park、Countryside；规划：bus station、shopping centre、car park、pedestrians only、dual carriageway、new housing 等
- **Task 2 题型**: advantage-disadvantage（outweigh 型）
- **Task 2 题干**: At the present time, the population of some countries includes a relatively large number of young adults, compared with the number of older people. Do the advantages of this situation outweigh the disadvantages?

### 剑桥12 Test 7
- **Task 1 类型**: bar chart（分组柱状图：2003-2013 年美国人去快餐店就餐频率）
- **Task 1 题干**: The chart below shows how frequently people in the USA ate in fast food restaurants between 2003 and 2013. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: 频率类别 Every day / Several times a week / Once a week / Once or twice a month / A few times a year / Never；年份 2003 / 2006 / 2013
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: In a number of countries, some people think it is necessary to spend large sums of money on constructing new railway lines for very fast trains between cities. Others believe the money should be spent on improving existing public transport. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑桥12 Test 8
- **Task 1 类型**: diagram（流程图：地热能发电）
- **Task 1 题干**: The diagram below shows how geothermal energy is used to produce electricity. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
- **Task 1 配图信息**: Geothermal power plant、cold water、steam、turbine、condenser 等环节（细节文本层不清）
- **Task 2 题型**: discussion
- **Task 2 题干**: Some people believe that allowing children to make their own choices on everyday matters (such as food, clothes and entertainment) is likely to result in a society of individuals who only think about their own wishes. Other people believe that it is important for children to make decisions about matters that affect them. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

## 剑桥雅思13（Academic）

### 剑桥13 Test 3

- **Task 2 题干**: Some people say History is one of the most important school subjects. Other people think that, in today's world, subjects like Science and Technology are more important than History. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑桥13 Test 4
- **Task 1 类型**: maps/plans（平面图：大学体育中心现状与改造后规划）

- **Task 2 题型**: problem-solution（two-part：原因 + 措施）
- **Task 2 题干**: In spite of the advances made in agriculture, many people around the world still go hungry. Why is this the case? What can be done about this problem?

### 观察要点
- discussion 是剑10-13 Task 2 的绝对主流（约四成），且集中在剑11、剑12。
- 混合题（如 agree/disagree + 追加问题、why + solution）实际作答时建议按两个问题分别回应。
- GT 书信仅剑10 有 2 道；剑11-13 为 Academic 独立成册，无 GT 套题。
