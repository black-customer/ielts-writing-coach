## 一、图表类型与结构规律

### 1.1 总原则：所有图表共用一个"骨架"

Simon 的方法高度统一——**永远 4 段、general to specific（由总到分）、永不写结论段**：

> "I always write 4 paragraphs:
> 1. Introduction - paraphrase the question
> 2. Overview - describe 2 main or general things
> 3. Details
> 4. Details"

> "If you read any of my example essays, you will see that I always write 4 paragraphs, and I use a 'general to specific' essay structure. … I don't write a conclusion because I have already summarised the information in paragraph 2. One reason I put the summary near the beginning (rather than at the end) is because I think it's easier to describe general things first, then specific things later."

为什么不写 conclusion（考官原话）：

> "A conclusion is really a final judgement, decision or opinion. This is perfect for the task 2 essay, but task 1 asks you to write a description without analysis or opinions. On the other hand, an 'overview' is a simple description of the main points."

四类数字图表（line graph / bar chart / pie chart / table）语言完全通用：

> "The language for the following 4 types of chart is the same: Line graphs, Bar charts, Pie charts, Tables. These 4 types of chart all show the same thing: numbers. … You need to be able to 1) describe numbers 2) compare different numbers 3) describe changes in numbers (increase, decrease etc.). Easy!"

只有一种题型需要"特殊语言"：

> "Only one type of question requires 'special' language: the process diagram. … A process diagram shows steps or stages, not numbers."

时间分配（每段 5 分钟）：

> "First 5 minutes: Read the question, make sure you understand the chart, write your introduction by paraphrasing the question. Second 5 minutes: Look at the chart and try to find 2 general points. … Final 10 minutes: Describe specific details. Try to break this part into 2 paragraphs."

### 1.2 各图型的"落地"差异

**Line graph（线图）**：重点在趋势 + 比较，严禁一条线写一段。

> "Line graphs always show changes over time. … For your summary paragraph, look at the 'big picture' — what changes happened to all of the lines from the beginning to the end of the period shown. … Never describe each line separately. The examiner wants to see comparisons. … The key years to describe are the first year and the last year. You should also mention any 'special' years (e.g. a peak or a significant rise/fall). … Don't use the passive (e.g. the number was increased), continuous (e.g. the number was increasing), or perfect tenses (e.g. the number has increased)."

细节段固定套路（Simon 自述"我线图永远这么写"）：

> "For line graphs, I always use the following approach:
> 1st detail paragraph: compare the numbers for the first year (e.g. 1951), then describe the changes up to a key point on the graph (e.g. peak numbers in 1971).
> 2nd detail paragraph: explain the general trend for each line after the key point (1971), then compare the numbers for the last year shown (2009)."

实证（法国电力线图，worksheet 21，band 9, 187 words）：第 3 段写 1980 年各来源对比 + "Just one year later, nuclear power overtook thermal power as the primary source of electricity."（交叉点）；第 4 段写 1980–2005 核电升到峰值 + 其余三条线到 2012 年的状态。

**Bar chart（柱图）**：先分清"变化型"还是"比较型"。

> "There are two types of bar chart: 1. those that show changes over time 2. those that compare different items. The important thing to remember about the second type is that you can't describe increases and decreases."
> "Charts show 'change' when we see TIME (usually years) on the x axis. Charts show 'comparison' when the x axis has different ITEMS rather than time. You can't talk about 'increase/decrease' for this type of chart."

比较型柱图分组法（交通事故柱图）：> "Details: I'd probably group together the two highest items (demand response and bus) in this paragraph. … Details: I'd group the three types of rail transport together for this paragraph. Write some nice comparison sentences!"

变化型柱图则按时间分段（房价柱图）：> "Summary: compare the two periods (prices fell overall from 1990-95, but rose from 1996-2002), and mention that London prices changed the most. Details: write one paragraph about each period."

**Pie chart（饼图）**：语言与柱图相同；细节段按"类别"或"年份"分组，绝不能一个国家/类别一段。

3 张饼（学校开支）按类别分两组：> "You can see that I chose to put the two types of salaries together in one paragraph, and the other three categories together in the second paragraph."

两张饼（也门/意大利人口）按年份分段、两国交叉比较：> "I separate the description of 'details' into two paragraphs: one about the first year, and one about the second year. I don't recommend writing a separate paragraph about each country (because this will mean that you don't compare the countries properly)."

**Table（表格）**：数字最多、最需要"挑选"。Simon 给出完整操作规则（2011-10-20 课）：

> "Before you start writing, highlight some key numbers. Choose the biggest number in each category in the table (i.e. in each column and row). If the table shows years, look for the biggest changes in numbers over the time period. You could also mention the smallest numbers, but you can ignore 'middle' numbers (neither biggest nor smallest).
> For your summary paragraph, try to compare whole categories (columns or rows) rather than individual 'cells' in the table. …
> In your two 'details' paragraphs, never describe each category (column or row) separately. The examiner wants to see comparisons. Try to organise the numbers you highlighted into 2 groups — one for each paragraph (e.g. highest numbers for all categories together, and lowest numbers together).
> Describe / compare the numbers you highlighted — include at least 3 numbers in each paragraph."

六国垃圾表格的分组示范：> "I would write one main paragraph comparing the 3 countries with the highest figures (US, Japan, Korea), and a separate paragraph about Ireland, Poland and Portugal. Try to use some 'comparing' language when describing the countries (e.g. while, whereas, by contrast) and some 'trend' language when describing the years (e.g. increased, rose, fell)."

**双图 / 多图**：核心口诀——"各写一段，不强行对比"。

> "How should you structure your task 1 report if the question shows two different charts (e.g. a line graph and a pie chart)? Answer: It's easy. Just follow the 4-paragraph structure below:
> Introduction - say what each chart shows (one or two sentences)
> Overview - write one sentence about each chart, describing it's main feature
> Describe the first chart in detail
> Describe the second chart in detail"

补充判断规则：> "If the units are different (e.g. one chart shows 'millions' and the other shows 'percentages'), you can't usually compare them. If the units are the same, you can probably compare the charts. If there are 3 or 4 charts, you can usually compare them."

3–4 张相关图要找"贯穿性总趋势"：> "You must give an overview of the information. This means that you need to find an overall trend that connects all 3 charts."（三张教育柱图范文即用 "the figures for developed countries are much higher than those for developing nations. Also, the charts show an overall increase…"）

**Process diagram（流程图）**：唯一"特殊语言"题型（steps 语言 + 被动语态）。

> "Try to write 4 paragraphs - introduction, summary of main points, 2 detail paragraphs. … For your summary, first say how many steps there are in the process. Then say where/how the process begins and ends (look at the first and last stages). … In paragraphs 3 and 4, describe the process step by step. Include the first and last steps that you mentioned in the summary, but try to describe them in more detail or in a different way. You could describe the steps in one paragraph, but it looks more organised if you break the description into two paragraphs. Just start paragraph 4 somewhere in the middle of the process. Mention every stage in the process. … Times (e.g. past dates) are not usually shown, so use the present simple tense. It's usually a good idea to use the passive…"

自然过程用主动、人造过程用被动：

> "We often use the active to describe a natural process and the passive to describe a man-made process."
> "Verbs will be active, not passive e.g. 'water evaporates', not 'water is evaporated'."

实证（蜜蜂生命周期，主动，169 words）：> "The life cycle of the honey bee begins when the female adult lays an egg … each egg hatches and the immature insect, or nymph, appears. … the nymph grows in size and sheds its skin three times."

**Comparison diagram（前后对比图/设计对比图）**：全程写"差异对比"，如美术馆改造图（将来时计划）：> "At present, visitors enter the gallery through doors which lead into a lobby. However, the plan is to move the entrance to the Parkinson Court side of the building, and visitors will walk straight into the exhibition area."

**Map（地图）**：分两类，写法分别向流程图和对比图看齐。

> "There are 2 types of map: A map that shows a comparison; A map that shows development of an area."
> "'Development map' - This is similar to a 'process diagram' because your task is to describe stages."
> "'Before and after map' - This is the same as a 'before and after diagram'."
> "'Locations map' - This is similar to a 'comparison diagram' because your task is to compare the locations."

地图 overview 的取点法：> "When comparing diagrams, we can count the number of changes and look for the main types of changes."

发展类地图按时间分段（Chorleywood 村）：1868–1883 一段、1883–1922 一段、1970–1994 一段，每段配一座新交通设施。选址类地图则两段都做双址对比：> "Don't write a separate paragraph about each site; it's much better to compare the sites."

## 二、Overview（概述段）规律

### 2.1 写法规则

- **位置**：紧跟 introduction 之后（也可放结尾，但他强烈建议放开头）。
- **句子数**：固定两句、两个"main/general points"。> "I write two overview sentences. A one-sentence overview isn't really enough."
- **不给具体数字**：> "Try not to include specific numbers in the overview. Save the specifics for later paragraphs."
- **看大局**：> "Look at the 'big picture' e.g. the overall change from the first year to the last year (if years are shown on the chart), the differences between whole categories rather than single numbers, or the total number of stages in a process."
- **重要性**：> "A current examiner recently told me that the most common mistake in students' task 1 essays is that there is no overview. … You won't get a high score if you don't write a good overview."
- **取点没有标准答案**：> "There are usually more than two main points to choose from, so don't worry about whether the examiner would choose something different."
- 数字图 overview 找什么：> "When the chart shows numbers, we look for the highest, lowest, biggest change, overall trend etc."
- 无数字图（流程/地图）overview 找什么：> "process diagram overview: The total number of steps in the process. Where the process begins and ends. … comparing diagram: The total number of changes or differences. The main changes or differences. The main similarities or what doesn't change."

### 2.2 八类图表的真实 overview 摘录

1. **Line graph（UK 通勤 1970–2030）**：
   > "It is clear that the car is by far the most popular means of transport for UK commuters throughout the period shown. Also, while the numbers of people who use the car and train increase gradually, the number of bus users falls steadily."
2. **Bar chart 比较型（奥运奖牌堆积图）**：
   > "It is clear that the USA is by far the most successful Olympic medal winning nation. It is also noticeable that the figures for gold, silver and bronze medals won by any particular country tend to be fairly similar."
3. **Bar chart 变化型（数字游戏销量）**：
   > "It is clear that sales of games for three out of the four platforms rose each year, leading to a significant rise in total global turnover over the 7-year period. Sales figures for handheld games were at least twice as high as those for any other platform in almost every year."
4. **Pie chart（学校开支 3 饼）**：
   > "It is clear that teachers' salaries made up the largest proportion of the school's spending in all three years (1981, 1991 and 2001). By contrast, insurance was the smallest cost in each year."
5. **Table（2012–13 旅游五国）**：
   > "It is clear that France was the world's most popular tourist destination in the years 2012 and 2013. However, the USA earned by far the most revenue from tourism over the same period."
6. **双图（柱图+饼图，夜校课程）**——一句一图：
   > "It is clear that significantly more women than men attended evening classes at the education centre. We can also see that evening courses were much more popular among older adults."
7. **Process（太阳能板）**——步数+起点终点：
   > "It is clear that there are five distinct stages in this process, beginning with the capture of energy from sunlight. The final two steps show how domestic electricity is connected to the external power supply."
8. **Map 发展型（Chorleywood）**：
   > "It is clear that the village grew as the transport infrastructure was improved. Four periods of development are shown on the map, and each of the populated areas is near to the main roads, the railway or the motorway."
9. **Map 前后对比（海岛度假村）**——数出变化数量 + 主要变化类型：
   > "It is clear that the island has changed considerably with the introduction of tourism, and six new features can be seen in the second diagram. The main developments are that the island is accessible and visitors have somewhere to stay."
10. **Map 选址型（Garlsdon 超市）**——主要差异+次要对比维度：
    > "The main difference between the two sites is that S1 is outside the town, whereas S2 is in the town centre. The sites can also be compared in terms of access by road or rail, and their positions relative to three smaller towns."

另附含未来年份饼图的 overview（预测并入概述）：> "It is clear that Italy had the older population in the year 2000, and the same is predicted for the year 2050. The populations of both countries are expected to age over the fifty-year period."

**Overview 常用开头套语**（全部出自范文）：It is clear that… / It is noticeable that… / It is also noticeable that… / We can see that… / Overall, …

## 三、开头段规律

原则：一句话、快、只做同义改写（paraphrase），不炫技：

> "Task 1 introductions should be fast and easy. Just paraphrase the question statement (rewrite it in your own words)."
> "Don't try to 'show off' in your introduction. Just use 'show', 'compare' or 'illustrate'; don't use words like 'depict' or 'indicate'."

对"十个 show 同义词"的警告：

> "I often meet students who have learnt ten different ways to paraphrase 'the graph shows'. … The answer is no. First, most synonyms of the word 'show' are not appropriate… The phrase 'the graph exhibits' will look very strange to the examiner. … I tell my students to remember just two synonyms for 'shows': compares and illustrates (e.g. the bar chart compares; the diagram illustrates)."

改写"换 3 个元素"示范：

> "The table below shows the figures for imprisonment in five countries between 1930 and 1980.
> table shows = bar chart compares; figures for imprisonment = number of people in prison; between... and... = over a period of
> The bar chart compares the number of people in prison in five different countries over a period of 50 years."

**开头动词变体清单（仅此三招 + give information）**，实际范文中的全部开头句型：
- The line graph **compares**… （最高频）
- The bar chart **compares**… / The chart **compares** A **in terms of** B
- The table **compares**… / The table **shows**… / The table **gives information about/on**…
- The pie charts **compare**…
- The diagrams **illustrate**… / The picture **illustrates**… / The figures **illustrate**…
- The first picture **shows**…, and the second **shows**… （双图分句式）
- The two pictures **compare**…

**Simon 官方改写素材库**（2012-06-28 与 2013-12-12 两课合并去重）：

```
graph = line graph
chart = bar chart / pie chart / flow chart / table
diagram = figure
shows = illustrates / compares
proportion = percentage
information = data
the number of = the figure for
the proportion of = the figure for
the total number = the overall number
people in the USA = Americans
US consumption = consumption in the United States
trends in = changes in
from 1999 to 2009 = between 1999 and 2009 = over a period of 10 years
in three countries = in the UK, France and Spain（点明国名）
how to produce = the process of producing
the process of paper recycling = how paper is recycled
to produce forecasts = to forecast
maps show = diagrams illustrate
an island before and after = some changes to an island
two possible sites = two potential locations
```

> "Tip: 'The figure for / figures for' is a great phrase that not many people use."

同一题三种不同开头示范（澳洲贫困表）：

> "1) The chart compares percentages of Australians from six different family types who were classed as poor in 1999.
> 2) The table gives information about poverty rates among six types of household in Australia in the year 1999.
> 3) The table compares different categories of Australian families in terms of the proportion of people living below the poverty line in each one."

双图开头可拆两句：> "The graph shows numbers of visits to the UK and trips abroad by UK residents. The bar chart shows the five most popular destinations for UK travellers."

## 四、细节段组织规律（第 3、4 段）

### 4.1 分组原则（按图表类型选用）

| 图型 | 分组依据 | 证据 |
|---|---|---|
| 线图 | 时间轴：首年对比→关键点；关键点后→末年对比 | "1st detail paragraph: compare the numbers for the first year, then describe the changes up to a key point…" |
| 柱图（比较型） | 大小排序：最大两项一段 / 其余一组 | "group together the two highest items… group the three types of rail transport together" |
| 柱图（变化型） | 时间分段：每时期一段 | "write one paragraph about each period" |
| 饼图（多年份） | 年份分段（同段内跨国/跨类比较） | "one about the first year, and one about the second year" |
| 饼图（多类别） | 类别归组（工资类 vs 其他三类） | "the two types of salaries together in one paragraph, and the other three categories together" |
| 表格 | 最高一组 vs 最低一组；或高数值国 vs 低数值国 | "highest numbers for all categories together, and lowest numbers together" |
| 双图 | 一图一段 | "write a separate paragraph about each" |
| 流程图 | 步骤中点切开："Just start paragraph 4 somewhere in the middle of the process" | 玻璃回收：生产 3 步一段、回收 5 步一段 |
| 地图（发展型） | 时间分期 | 1868–1883 / 1883–1922 / … / 1970–1994 |
| 地图（选址型） | 对比维度（位置 vs 交通） | "one paragraph comparing the position of each site relative to Garlsdon, and another paragraph about the positions relative to transport links" |

### 4.2 数据点数量：selective，不罗列

- 每个细节段约 **3 句话**：> "I usually find that 3 sentences are enough for a good 'details' paragraph, which means that we only need to choose 3 things to describe for each chart."
- 全文数字量：> "I usually mention around 6 or 7 numbers in my main paragraphs." / "include at least 3 numbers in each paragraph"（每段 ≥3 个数）。注：worksheets 标注 "word count includes all numbers"。
- 为什么必须取舍：> "The table below contains 36 numbers. We can't describe all of them, so which ones should we choose?" / "The following bar chart has a total of 24 bars. It's impossible to describe 24 pieces of information in only 20 minutes, so you need to select."
- 取舍规则：> "A simple rule is to select at least one key thing about each country."（每国/每类至少给一个关键点；最大值给数字，中间值可忽略）；> "Make sure that you write something about each country. Select the most relevant point for each country, and don't forget to mention some figures."
- 首尾年必写：> "The key years to describe are the first year and the last year. You should also mention any 'special' years (e.g. a peak or a significant rise/fall)."

### 4.3 真实细节段 + 功能标注

**例 1：线图（法国电力，worksheet 21）第 3 段——"起点横比 + 交叉点"**
> "In 1980, thermal power stations were the main source of electricity in France, generating around 120 terawatt hours of power. Nuclear and hydroelectric power stations produced just under 75 terawatt hours of electricity each, and renewables provided a negligible amount. Just one year later, nuclear power overtook thermal power as the primary source of electricity."
【功能：句1 首年排序+最大值；句2 其余线横比；句3 指出趋势反转的"特殊年"】

**例 2：表格（手机功能 2006–2010）第 3 段——"首年全表扫描 + 对比"**
> "In 2006, 100% of mobile phone owners used their phones to make calls, while the next most popular functions were text messaging (73%) and taking photos (66%). By contrast, less than 20% of owners played games or music on their phones, and there were no figures for users doing Internet searches or recording video."
【功能：最高值+括号内嵌数字；while 横比；By contrast 补低值与"无数据"项】

**例 3：表格（2012–13 旅游）第 4 段——"最高值 + 倍数比较 + 有趣例外"**
> "Spending by tourists visiting the USA increased from $126.2 billion in 2012 to $139.6 billion in 2013, and these figures were well over twice as high as those for any other country. Spain received the second highest amounts of tourist revenue, rising from $56.3 billion to $60.4 billion, followed by France, China and Italy. Interestingly, despite falling numbers of tourists, Chinese revenue from tourism rose by $1.7 billion in 2013."
【功能：冠军数据+倍数；亚军+排序收尾；指出反常点（游客降收入升）】

**例 4：饼图（3 饼，学校开支）细节段——"类别归组"**
> "In 1981, 40% of the school's budget went on teachers' salaries. This figure rose to 50% in 1991, but fell again by 5% in 2001. The proportion of spending on other workers' wages fell steadily over the 20-year period, from 28% of the budget in 1981 to only 15% in 2001."
【功能：单类三期走势写成一句主线；第二类用 over the period 概括降幅】

**例 5：双图（柱+饼，夜校）第 4 段——"切回第二张图 + 分段给数"**
> "Looking at the age profile pie chart, we can see that the majority of people attending evening lessons were over 40 years of age. To be precise, 42% of them were aged 50 or more, and 26% were aged between 40 and 49. Younger adults were in the minority, with only 11% of students aged 20 to 29, and only 5% aged under 20."
【功能：段首句明示"现在看第二张图"；先概括后给数；with + 数字收尾】

## 五、语言库（全部摘自原文）

### 5.1 趋势动词与两种句式

Simon 反对"夸张词"：

> "When describing changes I prefer to avoid words like soar, rocket and plummet because they are too 'sensationalist' - they exaggerate too much, and are more journalistic than academic in style. Instead, we can demonstrate good control of grammar by using words like increase, rise and fall as both nouns and verbs."

名词/动词双句式（原文例句）：

```
- London saw a significant increase in the cost of homes.        (noun)
- The cost of homes in London increased significantly.           (verb)
- There was a rise in house prices between 1990 and 1995.        (noun)
- House prices rose between 1990 and 1995.                       (verb)
- There was a 7% fall in the average house price in Tokyo.       (noun)
- The average Tokyo house price fell by 7%.                      (verb)
```

rise vs raise：> "something rises (e.g. the price rose); somebody raises something (e.g. the company raised the price)… 'Raise' is almost always a verb. You probably won't use it for task 1."

'see' 三变换：> "Britain saw a dramatic increase in CD sales in the 1980s. / The 1980s saw a dramatic increase in CD sales in Britain. / British shops saw CD sales increase dramatically in the 1980s."

**上升**（原文出现过）：rose (to/by), increased, jumped (to), doubled（避免 soar/rocket）, rose steadily/gradually/quickly/dramatically, saw an increase / there was a rise in…, "there was a significant/marked rise in…"
- "the percentage of people using their phones to access the Internet jumped to 41% in 2008 and then to 73% in 2010."
- "London house prices jumped to around 12% above the 1989 average."
- "waste production in the US rose from 131 to 192 million tonnes."

**下降**：fell (to/by), dropped (by/to), decreased, declined, "fell back to", "dropped to its lowest point, at around $2.5 billion", "went down by 5%", "fell steadily/gradually/dramatically".
- "Emissions from electricity, gas and water supply fell dramatically to only 0.5 million tonnes in 2007, a drop of almost 3 million tonnes."

**波动**：fluctuated (between… and…), "the figures for pork fluctuated between 50 and 40 pounds per person", "the number of people emigrating fluctuated", "the percentages for resources and furniture/equipment fluctuated."

**平稳/保持**：remained stable, remained steady, remained high, remained relatively stable, remained at a similar level, stayed roughly the same, "remained at this level for the rest of the period", "there was relatively little change in…", "remained below 5% until the early 2000s."

**峰值/交叉/反超**：reached a peak of, peaked at, "reached its peak in 2001, at 23%", "reached peaks of 17, 9 and 7 billion dollars respectively", "rose from around 60 to a peak of 90 pounds", "nuclear power overtook thermal power as the primary source", "Apple overtook the other two vendors in 2011", "The year 2006 marks the point at which expenditure on mobile services overtook that for residential phone services.", "figures eventually surpassing those for beef."

### 5.2 变化幅度副词（真实搭配）

- dramatically：rose/fell dramatically to a peak of 430…（"a dramatic fall in precipitation", "the most dramatic changes"）
- significantly：increased significantly; "significantly more money"; "significantly fewer injuries"; "serve significantly more passengers"
- considerably："fell considerably"; "has changed considerably"; "consumption was considerably higher in Brazil than in the Congo"; "rose considerably"（骑行车表 overview）
- markedly：a marked increase in the popularity of…
- slightly：rose slightly; "slightly higher figures"; "slightly lower, at about 25% and 23%"; "decreased slightly"
- steadily：fell steadily; rose steadily; "the number of bus users falls steadily"
- gradually：increase gradually; rose gradually
- rapidly：saw rapid growth
- quickly：expenditure rose quickly
- relatively 搭配："remain relatively stable", "remained relatively stable, at between 50 and 80 terawatt hours", "monthly temperatures remain relatively stable"
- approximately / around / about / just over / just under / nearly / roughly / close to / well over：数字修饰词（"just under 75 terawatt hours", "well over twice as high", "close to 450 million", "nearly three times as high"）

### 5.3 倍数与分数（原文专题 + 范文实例）

Simon 专题课（2012-11-01）三种形式：

```
1. 'double' (动词)
   The number of unemployed people doubled between 2005 and 2009.
2. 'twice as...as/compared to', 'three times as...as/compared to'
   There were twice as many unemployed people in 2009 as in 2005.
   Twice as many people were unemployed in 2009 compared to 2005.
3. 'twofold', 'threefold' (形容词/副词)
   There was a twofold increase in the number of unemployed people between 2005 and 2009. (形容词)
   The number of unemployed people increased twofold between 2005 and 2009. (副词)
```

范文中的实例：
- "Sales figures for handheld games were **at least twice as high as** those for any other platform."
- "These figures were **nearly three times as high as** those for the second highest category, bus services."
- "**almost double** the amount that any previous winner had eaten" / "roughly double the amount"
- "The second largest system, in Paris, is only **about half the size of** the London underground."
- "the figure for spending on residential services **had fallen to just over half this amount**."
- "New Zealanders spent **only half as much** in relative terms on recreation (17%) as UK residents (34%)."
- "waste production increased **more than eightfold**, from only 0.6 million tonnes…"
- "The figures for industrial and domestic water consumption **stood at around one fifth of that amount**."
- "**Just over one fifth of** single parents were living in poverty."
- "the number of scientists and technicians … **almost doubled** to about 70 per 1000 people."
- "the amount of rainfall **more than doubles** between May and June."
- "English homes use **around double the amount of** electricity in the winter compared to the summer."
- "The permanent exhibition space … will be **about twice as large as** it is now."
- "Brazil had **265 times more** irrigated land."

### 5.4 百分比与数字句式

百分比两句式+比较（Simon 专题课原文）：

```
1) 百分比放句首: 6% of single aged people were living in poverty.
2) 百分比放句尾: The level of poverty among single aged people stood at 6%.
3) 句尾加比较:   6% of single aged people were living in poverty, compared to only 4% of aged couples.
                 The level of poverty among single aged people stood at 6%, whereas the figure for aged couples was only 4%.
```

> "English speakers usually put the percentage at the start of the sentence. Use while, whereas or compared to (after a comma) to add a comparison. Use 'the figure for' to add another comparison in the next sentence."

核心动词搭配（全部出自范文/填空答案）：
- **account for**："agriculture accounted for the largest proportion of water used"; "Three kitchen appliances, namely ovens, kettles and washing machines, account for 17.5% of household electricity use"; "Leisure activities accounted for the highest proportion of UK household spending."
- **make up**："teachers' salaries made up the largest proportion of the school's spending"; "make up 60% of the healthy diet for sport"（填空答案 make…up / constitutes）
- **stand at**："worldwide sales of handheld games stood at around $11 billion"; "the figure for Japan stood at 28 million tonnes"; "the number of people who emigrated stood at just under 300,000."
- **go on / spend / pay out**："40% of the school's budget went on teachers' salaries"; "US consumers spent an average of nearly $700 on residential phone services"; "Italians paid out more for personal stereos."
- **was/were + 分数/比例**："In 2012, 83 million tourists visited France"; "Almost 100% of Canadians used the Internet."
- **give/contribute**："Food and non-alcoholic drink purchases contributed £51 to weekly household expenditure."
- **占比表达**："the largest proportion of electricity, 52.5%, is used for heating rooms and water"; "Coal was used to produce 50 of the total 100 units of electricity… rising to 130 out of 170 units in 2000."
- "totalled"："The amount of goods transported by road totalled 60 million tonnes."

### 5.5 比较句式

- **最高级 / by far**："the USA is by far the most successful…"; "commuter rail services recorded by far the lowest figures"; "London experienced by far the greatest changes"; "beef was by far the most popular of the four types of meat"; "the USA earned by far the most revenue"; "Turkey … was noticeably higher … than in any of the other countries."
- **while / whereas**（两个位置，Simon 专题）：

```
1) 'while' at the beginning:
   While the numbers of people who use the car and train increase gradually, the number of bus users falls steadily.
2) 'while' in the middle:
   In 1970, around 5 million UK commuters travelled by car on a daily basis, while the bus and train were used by about 4 million and 2 million people respectively.
```

- **compared to / with / in comparison with**："You can use 'compared to', 'compared with' and 'in comparison with' in the same way." 注意：> "We don't say 'comparing to'."
- **that/those 指代**："the figures for developed countries are much higher than **those** for developing nations"; "Water consumption per person in Brazil … was much higher than **that** in the Congo."
- **similar**："the figures for… tend to be fairly similar"; "all with similar proportions of each medal colour"; "Similar levels of household spending were seen in the West Midlands, the North West and the East Midlands."
- **second place**（仅限"竞赛型"图）："In second place on the all-time medals chart is the Soviet Union, with just over 1,000 medals." + 警告：> "Only use phrases like 'in second place' if the chart shows some kind of competition. Don't write 'in first / second place' if the chart shows unemployment or health problems!"
- **the only...**："China is the only other country with a noticeably higher proportion of gold medals"; "Korea was the only country that managed to reduce its waste output."
- **respectively**："about 10% and 5% respectively"; "with 8,108 in 2001 and 15,768 in 2011"; "at nearly 16% and just over 5% respectively."
- **数字+变化量同位语**："this figure rose to more than 106 thousand in 2011, **an increase of 144%**"; "The number of Japanese tourists who travelled abroad increased dramatically from just under 5 million to around 15 million between 1985 and 1995, **a rise of about 10 million in 10 years**"; "there was a small drop of approximately 0.5 million in the number of bus users."

### 5.6 时间表达

- **over the period (shown) / over a … period**："over a period of 60 years"; "over a 20-year period"; "over the 10-year period"; "over the 7-year period"; "over the course of a calendar year"; "over a period of 13 years from 1989"; "over the 28-year period."
- **between … and … / from … to …**："between 1980 and 2000"; "from 1999 to 2009"; "Between 9 and 10 days later"; "from 2030 to 2040."
- **in + 年份**："in 1990"; "in the year 2000"; "in the years 1980, 1991 and 2001"; "In the same year."
- **by + 年份**（配过去时或将来）："By 2000, global water use for agriculture had increased to around 3000km³"; "By 2030, the number of people who commute by car is expected to reach almost 9 million."
- **in vs by + 时态**（专题课）：

```
In 2002, the cost of an average house in the UK was around £130,000. By 2007, the average house price had risen to almost £190,000, but it fell back to just under £150,000 in 2008.
- I used "in" with the past simple (was, fell).
- I used "by" with the past perfect (had risen) to give the idea that the increase had happened in the years leading up to 2007.
- To avoid worrying about "by + past perfect" you could write: "Between 2002 and 2007, the average house price rose to..."
```

- **期间推移**："over the following five years"; "Over the next 3 years"; "over the next 30 years"; "During the same period"; "Throughout the period shown"; "for the majority of the period"; "until the early 2000s"; "from the late 1970's onwards."

### 5.7 预测语言（未来年份）

> "In 2015, the proportion of people using the Internet in the USA **is expected to be** about 20%. The figures for Canada and Mexico **are likely to be** lower, at about 10% and 5% respectively. In 2025, **it is predicted that** Internet usage in both the USA and Canada **will rise** to around 70% of the population, while the figure for Mexico **should reach** just over 25%."

范文实例：
- "the number of people who commute by car **is expected to reach** almost 9 million, and the number of train users **is also predicted to rise**, to nearly 5 million. By contrast, buses **are predicted to become** a less popular choice."
- "Japan **is expected to see** the most dramatic changes in its elderly population."
- "a sudden increase … **is predicted** for Japan"; "By 2040, **it is thought that** around 27% of the Japanese population **will be** 65 years old or more."
- "the same **is predicted** for the year 2050"; "The populations of both countries **are expected to age** over the fifty-year period."
- 老龄化线图（含 2040 未来年）概述中就用一般现在时概括整体趋势："It is clear that the proportion of elderly people **increases** in each country between 1940 and 2040. Japan **is expected to see** the most dramatic changes in its elderly population."（Simon 的 essay-analysis 课原注："Verbs - compares, increases, is expected to see, were, rose, remained... (look carefully at the use of tenses)"）

### 5.8 流程图语言

两大特征（Simon 原话）："There are 2 things that make process descriptions special: 1. Phrases that order the process and link the steps 2. Passive verbs."

**顺序连接语**（范文全集）：at the first stage in the process; at the first/second/third/fourth/sixth stage (of/in the process); at the fourth step shown on the diagram; at the final stage; next; then; after that; following this; finally; firstly / secondly; beginning with… ; and ends at…; from the initial collection of waste paper to the eventual production of usable paper.

**被动语态实例**：
- "waste paper **is collected** either from paper banks… This paper **is then sorted** by hand and **separated** according to its grade, **with** any paper that is not suitable for recycling **being removed**. Next, the graded paper **is transported** to a paper mill."
- "The paper **is cleaned and pulped**, and foreign objects such as staples **are taken out**. Following this, all remnants of ink and glue **are removed**… the pulp **can be processed** in a paper making machine."
- "sand, soda ash, limestone and other chemicals **are mixed** together… this mixture **is heated** in a glass furnace at approximately 1500℃… The molten glass **can then be shaped**, by blowing, to create the end products."
- "coffee beans **must first be picked**… These beans **are then dried, roasted, and cooled** before **being put** in a grinding machine."
- "the bricks **are heated** in a kiln at a moderate temperature (200 - 900 degrees Celsius)… Finally, the finished bricks **are packaged and delivered**."

**概述句式**（流程图 overview 固定写法）：
- "It is clear that there are **five distinct stages** in this process, beginning with… The final two steps show…"
- "It is clear that there are **11 stages** in the production of coffee. The process **begins with** the picking of coffee beans, and **ends at** the packing stage."
- "There are four main stages in the life cycle of the silkworm, from eggs to adult moth. The process of silk cloth production involves six steps, from silkworm cocoon to silk material."

**主动语态（自然过程/生命周期）**："the female adult lays an egg"; "each egg hatches"; "the nymph grows in size and sheds its skin three times"; "the young adult honey bee emerges from its final moulting stage"; "Ocean water evaporates, falls as rain, and eventually runs back into the oceans again."

### 5.9 地图语言

- **新建（过去时被动）**："in 1909 a railway line **was built** crossing this area from west to east"; "a motorway **was built** to the east of the village."
- **新建（现在完成时被动，前后对比图）**："small huts **have been built** to accommodate visitors"; "The other physical structures **that have been added** are a reception building…"
- **现状/位置**："Chorleywood Park and Golf Course **is now located** next to this original village area"; "the island **is accessible**"; "There is also a short road **linking** the pier with the reception…, and footpaths **connect** the huts"; "a designated swimming area for tourists **off a beach on the western tip** of the island"; "**in the middle of**, **to the north of**."
- **扩张/发展**："the village **grew along** the main road to the south"; "**The expansion of** Chorleywood **continued** to the east and west **alongside** the railway line"; "further development of the village **took place around** motorway intersections."
- **变化总述**："the island **has changed considerably** with the introduction of tourism, and **six new features can be seen** in the second diagram"; "The main developments are that…"; "Before these developments, the island was **completely bare apart from** a few trees."
- **规划/将来（building plans）**："significant changes **will be made** in terms of the use of floor space"; "**the plan is to** move the entrance…"; "visitors **will walk** straight into the exhibition area"; "a new set of winding stairs **will be installed**"; "the planned building work **will also include** the installation of some kitchen furniture."
- **方位介词**：to the north west of; close to; relative to; runs through; surrounds the town centre; lies on the main road.
- 时态混合（Simon 点评海岛图）："Range of verb tenses, and use of both active and passive: has been developed, have been built / has changed / can be seen / is, are, include / was."

## 六、语法与数字处理

### 6.1 时态选择（Simon 给出明确规则）

| 情形 | 时态 | 原文依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 图表给过去年份 | 一般过去时 | "Use the past simple for past years" |
| 图表给未来年份 | will / is expected to / is predicted to | "'will' or 'is expected/predicted to' for future years" |
| 无时间/平均值（如气候图、比例图） | 一般现在时 | "If no time is shown, use the present simple." / "the verbs used above are in the present simple (vary, remain, are, reaches, experiences) because the graph shows average figures, not particular years." |
| 流程图（通常无时间） | 一般现在时（被动为主） | "Times (e.g. past dates) are not usually shown, so use the present simple tense." |
| 自然过程 | 现在时主动 | "water evaporates, not water is evaporated" |
| by + 过去年份 | 过去完成时（可用 between…and…+过去时回避） | 见 5.6 |
| 地图"现在"对比 | 现在完成时 + 一般现在/过去混合 | "has been developed, have been built / has changed / was" |
| 规划图 | will / be going to（范文用 will） | "significant changes will be made" |

> "For IELTS writing task 1, don't worry about using 'difficult' verbs or verb tenses. Forget about continuous and perfect tenses; just use present or past simple."（线图禁用被动/进行/完成："Don't use the passive (e.g. the number was increased), continuous (e.g. the number was increasing), or perfect tenses (e.g. the number has increased)."）

### 6.2 数据取舍（selective）

见第四节。要点：首年、末年、特殊年（峰值/剧变）必写；每国/每类至少一个关键点；中间值可忽略；总量 6–7 个数字；每段 ≥3 个数、约 3 句话。

### 6.3 单位写法

- 数字 + 单数单位："131 **million tonnes**"（错：131 millions of tonnes / 11 millions）。> "When there is a number we say '131 million tonnes'. Only use 'millions of tonnes' when there is no number."
- 无数字时用复数："Millions of people travel abroad each year."
- > "Students often make mistakes with thousands, millions and billions. … you should say '10 million' not '10 millions'. It's the same with hundred, thousand and billion… examiners notice it!"
- 常见单位格式：$11 billion; £470 per week; 394 kilometres of route; 45,000 units; per capita / per person / per 100 million passenger miles; (in millions of tonnes) 括号单位照抄表格。
- 负百分比禁止：> "don't write -5%, write 'fell by 5%'."
- 百分号数字句必须落实为主语："In 2012, text message was 95%" 是 Simon 点名的"大错"（text message 不能"是"95%）。

### 6.4 number / amount / proportion / figure（Simon 语法专题原文）

```
number    - Use "the number of + plural noun" e.g. the number of visitors.
            Don't use it to describe percentages or uncountable nouns e.g. money.
amount    - Use "the amount of + uncountable noun" e.g. the amount of money.
            Don't use it with countable nouns e.g. the amount of person/people.
proportion- Only use this to describe percentages (not numbers).
            Use "the proportion of + plural noun" e.g. the proportion of people.
figure    - Use "the figure for + plural noun" e.g. the figure for visitors to the UK.
            Use it with uncountable nouns e.g. the figure for unemployment.
            Use it with countries e.g. the figure for Canada.
            Use it with percentages e.g. the figure (for...) rose to 10%.
```

### 6.5 数字句的动词陷阱

> "You can't say 'Canada was 19 million tonnes' or 'Australia was lower' or 'Canada increased' - the country didn't increase, the wheat exports increased."
Simon 修正版：
> "1. In 1985, Canada exported about 19 million tonnes of wheat. 2. Australia exported less wheat, at 15 million tonnes. 3. In 1988, Canadian wheat exports increased by about 5 million tonnes. 4. Australia exported about 11 million tonnes of wheat in 1990."

图表标签是单数、句中常需复数：

> "the labels on a chart could be: single parent, graduate, only child, laptop computer. But when you write a sentence, you might need to use a plural: The number of single parents increased. / In 1999 nearly 55% of graduates were female. … Don't just copy the words from the graph or chart."

### 6.6 to / by / with / at 四介词（专题课原文）

```
1) to   — 到达的新数值: In 2008, the rate of unemployment rose to 10%.
2) by   — 变化量:       In 2009, the rate of unemployment fell by 2% (from 10% to 8%).
3) with — "拥有"该数值: Obama won the election with 52% of the vote.
4) at   — 句尾补数值:   Unemployment reached its highest level in 2008, at 10%.
```

### 6.7 关键词重复与同义替换的度

- 重复不怕："It's impossible to avoid repeating some of the key words in writing task 1. Try to add variety where you can… but don't become obsessed with this. The important thing is to describe and compare the data."
- 小变体即可（原文清单）：average weekly spending → spent on average / average expenditure / weekly spending figures / levels of spending / spent per week / expenditure per week。
- 真正加分的是"同一事物的多样表达"：以 "the numbers of residents cycling to work" 为例，Simon 在一篇里写了 the numbers of people who cycled to work / the number of UK commuters who travelled to work by bicycle / the number of cycling commuters / residents commuted by bicycle / this figure / total numbers of cycling commuters / figures for。

## 七、字数与时长

- **最低 150 词，无上限，每个词都算**：
  > "You must write 150 words or more." / "Can I write more than 150 words? Yes. There is no maximum word count." / "Is every word counted, even words like 'a'? Yes. All words are counted."

Don't describe items separately… Don't try to describe every number on a chart or graph…"

## 八、中国考生常犯的 Task 1 错误清单（对照 Simon 的做法）

以下每条都来自 Simon 的"找错/改错"课或范文对比，均为考官亲眼所见的真实高频错误：

1. **不写 overview，或用一句话"结论"充当**。Simon：> "the most common mistake in students' task 1 essays is that there is no overview… A quick one-sentence conclusion is not good enough." 对策：第 2 段固定写两句、两个 main points、不带数字。
2. **开头照抄题目**。> "Don't copy the question for your introduction. You should paraphrase the question." 对策：只记 shows = compares / illustrates 两个替换 + 官方改写素材库（见第三节）。
3. **"国家/事物 + was + 数字"**（如 "Canada was about 19 million tonnes"、"The USA was the highest, at just over 4%"）。Simon：> "the country didn't increase, the wheat exports increased." 对策：用 exported/produced/earned 或 "the figure for Canada"。他称此为 "The most common mistake in IELTS writing task 1 is not a grammar mistake"（实为主谓逻辑错）。
4. **"10 millions / millions of tonnes 带数字使用"**。对策："10 million people"；只有无数字才用 millions of。
5. **百分比当主语**（"In 2012, text message was 95%, while email was only 52%."——Simon 原题）。对策："95% of users used their phones to make calls" 或 "The level of… stood at 6%"。
6. **该主动却用被动**（"The number of marriages was decreased…"、"the number was increased"）。Simon 判定：被动只属于流程图；线图趋势句禁被动/进行/完成时。
7. **时态错误**（过去年份用完成时/现在时："As from 1990 to 2000 marriage rate has decreased…"）。对策：过去年份一律 past simple；未来年份 is expected to / will。
8. **写 "-5%"**。对策：> "don't write -5%, write 'fell by 5%'."
9. **一条线/一个类别/一个国家单独写一段，全程无比较**。Simon：> "Never describe each line separately. The examiner wants to see comparisons." / "Always compare the countries; never describe the figures for each country in separate paragraphs."
10. **试图写完所有数字**（36 个数字的表格、24 根柱子）。对策：挑最大/最小/最大变化，中间值忽略；全文 6–7 个数字。
11. **非竞赛型图表用 "in first/second place"**（如失业率）。Simon：> "Don't write 'in first / second place' if the chart shows unemployment or health problems!"
12. **滥用 show 的"高级同义词"**（depicts, indicates, reveals, displays, exhibits）。Simon：> "none of these words are really appropriate… The phrase 'the graph exhibits' will look very strange to the examiner."
13. **用 soar / rocket / plummet 等夸张词**。对策：用 increase/rise/fall 的名动双句式展示语法控制力，"less common vocabulary" 留给话题词（global turnover, platform 等）。
14. **句子残缺或连接错误**（"While the divorce rate increased from 1 million to 1.5 million during the same period."——while 从句孤立；"As shown in table 1 that…"——杂糅）。对策：while 从句必须挂在主句上；写 "The first table shows that…"。
15. **rise/raise 混用**。对策：物作主语用 rise；raise 是及物动词，Task 1 几乎不用。
16. **忘记段落分隔**。> "Don't forget to separate your paragraphs clearly."（Simon 4 段之间空行。）
17. **给原因、做分析**（Task 1 加解释）。> "The description on the website is inappropriate for IELTS because there is too much analysis of reasons… don't try to give reasons in your IELTS essay!"（注意 Simon 本人在 band 9 范文中也仅写了个别事实性归因，如 "this could be explained by the fact that Brazil had 265 times more irrigated land"；普通考生不要模仿展开原因分析。）
18. **单复数照抄图例**（标签 single parent → 句中应说 single parents）。见 6.5。
19. **comparing to**（不存在）。> "We don't say 'comparing to'." 用 compared to / compared with / in comparison with，数字对比优先 while/whereas。
20. **过度纠结同义替换导致语义漂移**。> "If you try to use too much variety, there is a danger that you will confuse the reader or write something that does not mean what you want it to mean."

## 附：Simon 式写作流程（可背诵的检查清单，源自 2014-11-27 "checklist" 课）

> "Know what the six types of question are (e.g. line graph...).
> Try several real test examples of each type.
> Know the 4-paragraph method suggested in lessons on this site.
> Practise paraphrasing the question to write introductions.
> Understand why we don't write a conclusion for task 1.
> Know how to write an 'overview', and what to include in this paragraph.
> Practise selecting key information, rather than describing everything.
> Be able to write good 'comparing' sentences.
> Be able to describe changes and trends (e.g. increase, decrease).
> Be able to use the passive to describe steps in a process."

考场上依次回答四个问题即可成文：
1. Introduction：图表说了什么？（改写题干一句话）
2. Overview：最显眼的两个总体特征是什么？（两句，无数字）
3. Detail 1：第一组细节（首年/最大值/第一张图/前半流程）
4. Detail 2：第二组细节（末年/对比/第二张图/后半流程）
