# 04 · Task 1 小作文完全指南
> 来源：Simon Task 1 全部课程与 22 篇 band 9 范文规律分析（完整引文证据见 knowledge/digests/小作文范文规律.md）。

---

## 1. 总骨架：永远 4 段，永不写结论段

```
① Introduction   改写题干，一句话，5分钟
② Overview       两句、两个总体特征、不带具体数字，5分钟
③ Details 1      约3句，≥3个数字，5分钟
④ Details 2      约3句，≥3个数字，5分钟
```

- **不写 conclusion**：Task 1 只描述不评价；overview 已经是"总结"（考官原话：conclusion 是 final judgement，属于 Task 2；Task 1 要的是 overview）。
- **Overview 是生死线**：现任考官告诉 Simon，考生小作文最常见的错误就是没有 overview。没有清晰 overview，TA（任务达成）上不了 6。
- **20 分钟**：Task 1 占写作总分 1/3，Task 2 占 2/3，绝不超时。
- 字数：≥150 无上限；Simon 的 band 9 范文实测 150–206 词，**160–190 是安全带**。
- 四类数字图（线/柱/饼/表）**语言完全通用**；只有流程图需要"特殊语言"（顺序词+被动）。
- **9 句法**（Simon 2019 课）：Task 1 全文 9 句 = 开头 1 + overview 2 + 细节 3+3。
- 写 overview 前先自问两个问题：**整体上什么在升/降？哪一组最大/最小/最显著？**
- 改写的真相（Simon 名言）："**verbs are easy; noun phrases are long and difficult**"——动词好改写，名词短语又长又难；同义词替换行不通时，用**搭配变体**替代（如 poverty lines → people living below the poverty line → poverty rates），别硬造同义词。
- 别碰统计术语："linear growth / exponential growth" 这类词不要用；小作文用简单但准确的语言即可拿高分。

## 2. Overview 怎么写（两句公式）

**句式**：`It is clear that + 特征1. / It is also noticeable that + 特征2.`（或 We can also see that... / Overall,...）

**找什么**（按图表类型）：

| 图型 | Overview 找 | 真实示例（Simon 范文原句） |
|---|---|---|
| 线图 | 谁最高/最低；谁升谁降（从首年到末年看大局） | "It is clear that the car is by far the most popular means of transport... while the numbers of people who use the car and train increase gradually, the number of bus users falls steadily." |
| 柱图（比较型） | 谁遥遥领先；各类之间相似性 | "It is clear that the USA is by far the most successful Olympic medal winning nation." |
| 柱图（变化型） | 整体趋势；最大与最小 | "Sales figures for handheld games were at least twice as high as those for any other platform in almost every year." |
| 饼图 | 最大占比者；最小占比者 | "It is clear that teachers' salaries made up the largest proportion of the school's spending in all three years. By contrast, insurance was the smallest cost in each year." |
| 表格 | 跨类别比较（不是单个格子） | "It is clear that France was the world's most popular tourist destination... However, the USA earned by far the most revenue." |
| 双图 | 一句一图 | "It is clear that significantly more women than men attended evening classes. We can also see that evening courses were much more popular among older adults." |
| 流程图 | **步数 + 起点 + 终点** | "It is clear that there are five distinct stages in this process, beginning with the capture of energy from sunlight." |
| 地图（发展型） | 发展主线（如随交通扩张） | "It is clear that the village grew as the transport infrastructure was improved." |
| 地图（对比型） | 变化数量 + 主要变化 | "...six new features can be seen in the second diagram. The main developments are that the island is accessible and visitors have somewhere to stay." |

规则：**不带具体数字**（数字留给细节段）；取点没有标准答案，别纠结考官会不会选别的。

## 3. 开头段：一句话，只做安全改写

- 只用 3 个动词替换 show：**compares / illustrates**（+ gives information about）。**不要用 depicts/indicates/reveals/exhibits**——考官觉得奇怪。
- 改写公式：换动词 + 换核心词（the number of ↔ the figure for；proportion ↔ percentage）+ 换时间表达（between 1999 and 2009 ↔ over a 10-year period）。
- 示例：`The table below shows the figures for imprisonment in five countries between 1930 and 1980.` → `The bar chart compares the number of people in prison in five different countries over a period of 50 years.`
- 双图可拆两句：`The graph shows... The bar chart shows...`

---

## 4. 细节段：按图型选分组法（核心 selectivity）

| 图型 | 细节段分组法 |
|---|---|
| 线图 | **永远不一条线一段！** 段1：首年横向对比 → 变化写到关键点（峰值/交叉年）；段2：关键点之后到末年的对比 |
| 柱图 | 先判断：X轴是年份=变化型（按时间分段）；X轴是项目=比较型（最大两项一段，其余一段，**禁写升降**） |
| 饼图 | 多年份→按年分段（同段内跨国比较）；多类别→按类别归组（**绝不一个国家一段**） |
| 表格 | 挑每行/列最大最小值，"最高一组一段 + 最低一组一段"，中间值忽略 |
| 双图 | 一图一段，单位不同就不强行对比 |
| 流程图 | 步骤一个不漏，从**中点**切成两段 |
| 地图（发展型） | 按时间分期；地图（选址/对比型）按比较维度，两址同段对比 |

数据取舍铁律：
- 全文 **6–7 个数字**，每个细节段 ≥3 个数字、约 3 句话。
- 首年、末年、特殊年（峰值/剧变/交叉）必写；每国/每类至少一个关键点；**中间值直接忽略**。
- "select at least one key thing about each country" —— 每个国家都要照顾到。

## 5. 语言库（背这一节就够）

### 5.1 趋势动词（名动双句式——语法控制力的展示）
- 动词式：`The cost of homes increased significantly.` / `House prices rose between 1990 and 1995.` / `The rate fell by 7%.`
- 名词式：`There was a significant increase in the cost of homes.` / `London saw a dramatic rise in house prices.`
- 上升：rose (to/by), increased, jumped (to), doubled ｜ 下降：fell, dropped, decreased, declined ｜ 波动：fluctuated between...and... ｜ 平稳：remained stable/steady, stayed roughly the same
- 峰值/反超：reached a peak of / peaked at / **overtook**（"nuclear power overtook thermal power as the primary source"）
- **禁用夸张词**：soar, rocket, plummet（太新闻化）；**禁用**线图被动/进行时/完成时（the number was increased ❌）

### 5.2 幅度副词
dramatically / significantly / considerably / markedly / rapidly / steadily / gradually / slightly / relatively + approximately, around, just over, just under, nearly, roughly, well over

### 5.3 倍数（三种式）
1. `The number doubled between 2005 and 2009.`
2. `There were twice as many unemployed people in 2009 as in 2005.` / `...nearly three times as high as those for...`
3. `There was a twofold increase in...` / `...increased eightfold`

### 5.4 百分比句式
- 句首：`6% of single aged people were living in poverty, compared to only 4% of aged couples.`
- 句尾：`The level of poverty among single aged people stood at 6%, whereas the figure for aged couples was only 4%.`
- 核心动词：**account for / make up / stand at** / go on / total

### 5.5 比较句式
- by far the most/lowest：`the USA is by far the most successful...`
- while/whereas 两个位置：句首 `While car use increases, bus use falls.`；句中 `...around 5 million travelled by car, while the bus and train were used by about 4 million and 2 million respectively.`
- that/those 指代：`the figures for developed countries are much higher than those for developing nations`
- compared to/with（没有 comparing to！）；similar levels / fairly similar

### 5.6 数字四介词
- **to** 到达值：rose to 10% ｜ **by** 变化量：fell by 2% ｜ **with** 拥有值：won with 52% ｜ **at** 补数值：reached its highest level in 2008, at 10%

### 5.7 时间与时态
- 过去年份→过去式；未来年份→is expected to / is predicted to / will；无年份（比例/均值/流程）→现在式
- `in 2002 ... was`；`by 2007 ... had risen to`（by+过去完成；也可用 between...and... 回避）
- over the period shown / over a 10-year period / throughout the period / from...to... / between...and...

### 5.8 流程图（唯一特殊语言）
- Overview：步数+起终点：`There are 11 stages in the production of coffee. The process begins with the picking of coffee beans, and ends at the packing stage.`
- 顺序词：at the first stage / next / then / after that / following this / finally
- 人造过程→**被动**：`The paper is then sorted by hand and separated according to its grade.` `The bricks are heated in a kiln... Finally, the finished bricks are packaged and delivered.`
- 自然过程/生命周期→主动：`The female adult lays an egg... each egg hatches... the nymph grows in size and sheds its skin three times.`

### 5.9 地图
- 新建（过去）：`a railway line was built crossing this area`；前后对比图用现在完成：`small huts have been built`
- 变化总述：`the island has changed considerably with the introduction of tourism` / `The main developments are that...`
- 规划图（将来）：`significant changes will be made` / `the plan is to move...` / `will be installed`
- 方位：to the north of / in the middle of / runs through / lies on / is located next to / alongside
- **地图题三种衔接**（Simon 归纳）：① 普通连接词；② 代词与限定词（this area / the new road）；③ 同义替换——三种交替使用。

### 5.10 主谓逻辑（Task 1 第一大错）
- ❌ `Canada was 19 million tonnes.` ❌ `Canada increased.` → ✅ `Canada exported about 19 million tonnes of wheat.` ✅ `Canadian wheat exports increased by 5 million tonnes.`
- 单位：`131 million tonnes`（有数字用单数）；无数字才说 `millions of tonnes`；不写 `-5%`，写 `fell by 5%`
- number of + 可数复数 / amount of + 不可数 / proportion of + 百分比 / **the figure for**（万能，加分项）
- 图例标签是单数，句子常要变复数（single parent → single parents）

## 6. Task 1 考场流程 + 自查

**20 分钟节奏**：读图找 overview（5min）→ intro 改写（5min）→ 细节两段（10min）。

**交卷七查**：
1. 有 overview 吗？两句、无数字？
2. 开头是不是照抄题目了？
3. 有没有一条线/一个国家单独成段（=没比较）？
4. 主谓逻辑："国家 was 数字" "国家 increased" 清零？
5. 时态对吗（过去年→过去式）？单位 10 millions ❌？
6. 数字 ≥6 个、每段 ≥3 个？中间值忽略了没？
7. 段落之间空行分开？

## 7. 六种图型的第一反应口诀

```
线图：首年对比 → 关键点 | 关键点后 → 末年（禁逐线写）
柱图：先问有没有年份（有=趋势图语言；无=只比较，禁升降）
饼图：按年分组或按类归组，绝不一国一段
表格：只挑最大最小，中间全扔
双图：一句一图一段一图，单位不同不硬比
流程：步数+起终点 overview，中点切段，人造用被动
地图：发展按时间分期；选址两址同段对比
```
