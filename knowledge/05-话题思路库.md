# 第一部分 书籍方法论：Simon 让你怎么准备 ideas

## 1.1 这本书为谁而写
- 原话："Many students have no ideas or opinions about IELTS writing topics. Even if your grammar is perfect, you will not get a high IELTS score if you do not know what to write."
  ——很多考生对话题毫无观点；语法再完美，不知道写什么也拿不到高分。
- 本书目标："This book is for students who want to go into the writing exam feeling confident that they have excellent ideas, opinions and vocabulary for as many topics as possible."
  ——让考生进考场时对尽可能多的话题都备好了观点、立场和词汇。

## 1.2 高分靠什么（他反对背模板、套话）
- 原话："The key to a high score is not grammar, it is not your use of words like 'moreover' or 'in addition', and it is not your use of phrases like 'this is a controversial issue nowadays'."
  ——高分的关键不是语法，不是 moreover / in addition 这类连接词，也不是 "this is a controversial issue nowadays" 这类空话套话。
- 原话："To get a high score you need to use good 'topic language' (words and phrases that are related to the question topic). You need good ideas in order to answer the question well."
  ——高分需要"话题语言"（与题目话题直接相关的词句），并且需要好观点才能答好题。

## 1.3 三条备考建议（Simon 原话）
1. **备考多于刷题（Spend more time preparing than testing）**
   - "When you test yourself, you find out what your level is, but you do not learn anything new. You will not improve if you only write test essays."
     ——只测试不会带来进步；写作文之前先研究话题、准备观点、立场和词汇。
   - "Before writing an essay, study the topic and prepare your ideas, opinions and vocabulary."
2. **偷我的观点（Steal my ideas!）**
   - "If you only use the vocabulary that you already know, you will not learn anything new, and you will not improve."
     ——只用已会的词和想法，就永远学不到新东西。
   - "Practise linking my topic ideas together to write 'perfect essays'."
     ——练习把书里的观点串起来写成"完美文章"。
3. **配合网站使用（Use this book together with the website）**
   - "The website ielts-simon.com contains lessons and videos that show you how to use the ideas from this book."

## 1.4 ideas 的组织方式（本书的目录逻辑）
```
话题大类（24 个）
  └─ 子话题（把一个大话题按"常见争议问题"拆开，如 Education 下分：
     studying abroad / technology in education / home-schooling / streaming ...）
      └─ 每个子话题内按【正方观点 → 反方观点 → 中立立场/解决方案】罗列短句
         ——每句就是一个 idea，可直接撑起一个主体段（main paragraph）
```
- 子话题本身就是"考题切入点"：书中一个子话题 ≈ 一类真题问法（如 "Positives/Negatives of Zoos" ↔ 动物园利弊题）。
- 大量子话题成对出现（Advantages / Disadvantages、for / against、causes / solutions），正反弹药都给。

## 1.5 拿到题目怎么用这些 ideas（family-history 补充材料的现场示范）
- **先判断题型**：Simon 把家族史题称为 "2-part question"，并强调："The task is NOT to write an essay about the positives and negatives of researching family history. The task is to explain why people do it, and then give your opinion as to whether it is a positive OR negative thing to do."
  ——先看清题目到底问什么，不要答偏。
- **10 分钟列提纲（Essay Plan - 10 minutes）**：
  - P1 只写两句：引出话题 + 对两个问题给出总回答；
  - P2 回答 why（列 3 个左右原因）；
  - P3 回答 positive or negative（给立场 + 2~3 个论据）；
  - P4 结论：换说法复述对两个问题的总回答。
- **做话题词汇研究**："Why not do some research on the Internet around the topic of 'family history'? Try to find some good words and phrases that English speakers use when writing about this topic" e.g. family tree, ancestors, genealogy, tracing family history, a person's roots.
  ——备考时就按话题积累"话题语言"。

# 第二部分 万能论证角度清单（论证"弹药"总纲）

> 以下 16 个角度全部从书中观点归纳而来，每条附书中原句作为锚点。任何话题都可以先扫一遍这张清单，对每个角度问一句："这件事在这个角度上会带来什么好处/坏处？"通常立刻能得到 3~5 个观点。

| # | 论证角度 | 书中锚点例句（证明该角度来自书中观点） |
|---|---------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | 金钱 / 经济（成本、收入、就业、投资、利润） | "Tourists spend money." / "It avoids the cost of imprisonment." / "This industry creates jobs and wealth." |
| 2 | 时间 / 便利（省时、随时随地、灵活） | "Modern public transport can be fast and cheap." / "It gives us instant access to information on almost any subject." |
| 3 | 健康（饮食、运动、压力、成瘾） | "People who walk or cycle regularly are generally healthier." / "Too much work can result in stress and poor health." |
| 4 | 环境（污染、资源、栖息地、废物） | "Public transport can help to reduce pollution in cities." / "GM crops might change whole ecosystems." |
| 5 | 效率（资源利用、生产力、能源节约） | "Cars and road space are not used efficiently." / "Recycling saves energy and raw materials." / "Happy, healthy workers are more productive." |
| 6 | 公平 / 平等（机会均等、同工同酬、可及性） | "Everyone has access to the same quality of care and treatment." / "Foreign and local workers should have the right to equal pay and conditions." |
| 7 | 心理 / 情感（快乐、压力、自尊、动机、归属感） | "Streaming could damage students' self esteem." / "Unemployment causes frustration and stress." / "Wealth does not lead to happiness." |
| 8 | 安全 / 犯罪（威慑、保护、事故风险） | "They are put in prison to ensure the safety of other citizens." / "This deters criminals." |
| 9 | 教育 / 知识 / 技能 | "Education gives people knowledge and skills." / "Zoos are educational, interesting and fun." |
| 10 | 权利 / 自由 / 隐私 / 伦理 | "Many people think that this surveillance violates our privacy." / "We have no right to take another human life." / "Humans have no moral right to do experiments on animals." |
| 11 | 文化 / 传统 / 身份 / 多元 | "It is important to maintain our different cultural identities." / "Immigration also creates cultural diversity." |
| 12 | 质量 / 选择 / 体验（服务品质、舒适度、多样性） | "It helps to improve quality while bringing prices down." / "Without advertising we would have less choice." |
| 13 | 责任主体（政府/企业/学校/家长/个人各该做什么） | "Parents must support the school rules." / "Companies should make goods that last longer." / "Individuals should also try to be greener." |
| 14 | 长远 vs 短期 | "Richer nations can help by investing in long-term projects." / "A sports career may only last 10 years." |

| 16 | 依赖 / 惰性风险（福利依赖、技术依赖、政府依赖） | "It can be argued that state housing creates dependence on the government." / "People rely too much on computers." |

**用法提示**：正方角度与反方角度常常对称——"省钱"对"浪费纳税人的钱"，"安全"对"侵犯自由"，"效率"对"失业/剥夺基本功"。想出一边后，立刻检查对称角度是否成立，正反观点就都有了。

## 1. Advertising 广告

【问法方向】（对应书中子话题）
- 广告的利与弊？(Positives / Negatives of Advertising)
- 是否应监管、控制广告，尤其针对儿童的广告？(Opinions about Advertising)

### 正方：Positives of Advertising
- Advertising is a key part of modern business. ——广告是现代商业的关键组成部分。
- Companies need to tell customers about their products. ——公司需要让顾客了解自己的产品。
- Advertisements inform us about the choices we have. ——广告让我们知道自己有哪些选择。
- Advertising is a creative industry that employs many people. ——广告是雇用大量人员的创意产业。
- Without advertising we would have less choice. ——没有广告，消费者的选择会更少。
- Without advertising there would be higher unemployment. ——没有广告，失业率会更高。
- Advertising is a form of modern art. ——广告是一种现代艺术形式。
- People enjoy adverts. ——人们喜欢看广告。

### 反方：Negatives of Advertising
- Advertising manipulates people. ——广告操纵人心。
- It aims to persuade people that buying a product will make them happier. ——它试图让人相信"买了就更快乐"。
- Advertisers focus on selling a brand image. ——广告商专注于推销品牌形象。
- They use glamorous, successful people. ——他们启用光鲜亮丽的成功人士（做代言）。
- We now live in a consumer culture. ——我们如今生活在消费主义文化中。
- We are persuaded to follow the latest trends. ——我们被劝着去追逐最新潮流。
- We are encouraged to associate certain brands with a higher status. ——我们被引导把某些品牌与更高社会地位挂钩。
- Advertisers often aim their marketing at children. ——广告商常把营销瞄准儿童。
- Children can easily be influenced by advertisements. ——儿童很容易被广告影响。
- Children put pressure on parents to buy them things. ——孩子会逼父母给他们买东西。

### 中立立场：Opinions about Advertising
- Advertising should be regulated. ——广告应当受到监管。
- Advertising aimed at children should be controlled or even banned. ——针对儿童的广告应受控制甚至禁止。
- Unhealthy foods should not be marketed in a way that attracts children. ——不健康食品不应以吸引孩子的方式营销。
- Products that can be a risk to health should display warnings. ——有健康风险的产品必须印警示。
- In some countries it is illegal to advertise cigarettes on television. ——在一些国家，电视播香烟广告是违法的。
- Warnings must be displayed on cigarette packets. ——烟盒上必须印警示语。
- However, advertising is necessary in free market economies. ——然而，广告在自由市场经济中是必需的。
- It creates demand for products. ——广告创造产品需求。
- Governments should only censor false information or products that are harmful. ——政府只应审查虚假信息和有害产品。

【常用论证角度】经济（就业、需求、选择）、心理（操纵、身份地位）、儿童保护、监管边界。

## 2. Animal Rights 动物权利

【问法方向】
- 动物实验该不该被支持？(Arguments for / against Animal Testing)
- 素食主义值得提倡吗？(Arguments for / against Vegetarianism)
- 动物园的利与弊？(Positives / Negatives of Zoos)

### 正方：Arguments for Animal Testing
- Animals are used in important scientific research. ——动物被用于重要的科学研究。
- It is necessary to do medical tests on new drugs. ——新药必须先做动物医学试验。
- Animal testing helps to advance medical and scientific knowledge. ——动物实验推动医学与科学进步。
- Many important medical discoveries involved experimentation on animals. ——许多重大医学发现都离不开动物实验。
- Researchers aim to minimise the suffering that animals experience. ——研究者会尽量减少动物的痛苦。
- Testing for the cosmetics industry is now banned in many countries. ——化妆品动物实验在许多国家已被禁止（说明滥用已被控制）。

### 反方：Arguments against Animal Testing
- The benefits of research using animals do not justify the suffering caused. ——动物研究的收益不能为动物承受的痛苦正名。
- There are alternative methods of research. ——存在替代性研究方法。
- The lives of animals should be respected. ——动物的生命应当被尊重。
- Humans have no moral right to do experiments on animals. ——人类没有拿动物做实验的道德权利。

### 正方：Arguments for Vegetarianism
- Vegetarians do not eat foods that are produced by killing animals. ——素食者不吃以杀戮动物为代价的食物。
- Many people choose a vegetarian diet for moral or health reasons. ——很多人出于道德或健康原因选择素食。
- A healthy diet is possible without eating meat. ——不吃肉也能吃得健康。
- It is unnecessary to kill animals for food. ——为食物杀死动物并非必要。
- A vegetarian diet may reduce the risk of diseases like cancer. ——素食或可降低癌症等疾病风险。
- Many people question the treatment of animals in factory farms. ——许多人质疑工厂化养殖对待动物的方式。

### 反方：Arguments against Vegetarianism
- Vegetarians do not eat a balanced diet. ——素食者的饮食不够均衡。
- In many cultures, meat is the main ingredient in traditional meals. ——许多文化中肉类是传统饮食的主角。
- Meat-eaters argue that animals are below humans in the food chain. ——食肉者认为动物在食物链上低于人类。
- It is completely natural for us to kill them for food. ——为食物而杀动物完全符合自然。
- Our aim should be to improve farming methods. ——我们该做的是改进养殖方式。
- Farms should produce organic food. ——农场应生产有机食品。

### 正方：Positives of Zoos
- Zoos play an important role in wildlife conservation. ——动物园在野生动物保护中作用重大。
- They can help to protect endangered species. ——动物园有助于保护濒危物种。
- Zoos allow scientists to study animals and their behaviour. ——动物园让科学家能研究动物及其行为。
- Zoos are educational, interesting and fun. ——动物园有教育性、有趣、好玩。
- Children, in particular, enjoy learning about animals. ——孩子尤其喜欢在动物园了解动物。
- Zoos provide job opportunities. ——动物园提供就业机会。

### 反方：Negatives of Zoos
- Zoo animals are kept in artificial environments. ——动物园把动物关在人工环境里。
- They are kept in cages or have limited space. ——它们被关在笼中或空间有限。
- Zoo animals rely on humans. ——动物园的动物依赖人类生存。
- They lose the freedom to hunt for food. ——它们失去了觅食的自由。
- The best way to save endangered species is by protecting natural habitats. ——保护濒危物种最好的办法是保护其自然栖息地。
- Some people believe that zoos are unethical. ——有人认为动物园不道德。
- Zoos exhibit animals with the aim of making money. ——动物园展出动物是为了赚钱。
- We have no right to use animals for entertainment and profit. ——人类无权把动物用作娱乐和牟利工具。

【常用论证角度】伦理与权利、科学/医学进步、健康、自然保护、教育与娱乐。

## 3. Cities 城市

【问法方向】
- 人们为什么涌入城市？城市化原因？(Reasons for Urbanisation)
- 城市生活的问题/缺点？(Negatives of City Life)
- 市中心步行街/禁车区的利与弊？(Pedestrian Areas)

### 城市化的原因：Reasons for Urbanisation
- People move to cities in search of job opportunities. ——人们为找工作而迁入城市。
- Cities offer greater employment possibilities and a higher standard of living. ——城市提供更多就业机会和更高生活水平。
- People migrate to cities from the countryside. ——人们从农村迁往城市。
- Traditional activities like farming need fewer workers nowadays. ——如今像农业这样的传统行当需要的劳动力更少。

### 反方：Negatives of City Life
- Life in cities has its drawbacks. ——城市生活也有缺陷。
- The cost of living is higher than in rural areas. ——城市生活成本高于农村。
- Some people do not manage to find work. ——有些人在城市找不到工作。
- Housing is usually much more expensive. ——住房通常贵得多。
- Homelessness and poverty are common in cities. ——无家可归和贫困在城市很常见。
- There is a gap between the rich and poor. ——贫富差距悬殊。
- Life in cities can be extremely stressful. ——城市生活压力极大。
- There are problems like traffic congestion and crime. ——还有交通拥堵和犯罪等问题。
- Cities lack a sense of community. ——城市缺乏社区感。
- People do not even know their neighbours. ——人们甚至不认识邻居。
- Cities are sometimes described as "concrete jungles". ——城市有时被称作"水泥丛林"。

### 解决方向：Pedestrian Areas（步行区）
- Pedestrian zones in city centres can improve the local environment. ——市中心的步行区能改善当地环境。
- Banning cars encourages people to walk or cycle. ——禁车鼓励人们步行或骑车。
- Many European cities have built bicycle lanes. ——许多欧洲城市修建了自行车道。
- Dependence on cars is linked to health problems like obesity. ——依赖汽车与肥胖等健康问题相关。
- People who walk or cycle regularly are generally healthier. ——经常步行或骑车的人普遍更健康。
- Pedestrian areas are safer and more attractive for both residents and tourists. ——步行区让居民和游客都更安全、更有吸引力。

【常用论证角度】经济机会 vs 生活成本、健康、环境、社区感/心理、安全。

## 4. Crime 犯罪

【问法方向】
- 如何预防犯罪？警察该扮演什么角色？(Police and Crime Prevention)
- 监狱的利弊、改造 vs 惩罚？(Punishments / Prisons / Rehabilitation)
- 该不该保留死刑？(Capital punishment)
- 社区服务能否替代监禁？(Community service)
- 媒体对犯罪的报道有何影响？(Crime in the Media)

### 警察与犯罪预防：Police and Crime Prevention
- The job of the police is to catch criminals. ——警察的职责是抓捕罪犯。
- They must also prevent crime and make communities safer. ——他们也必须预防犯罪、让社区更安全。
- There should be an increase in the number of police officers on the streets. ——街头警力应当增加。
- Police officers should be seen as part of the community. ——警察应被视为社区的一员。
- They should be involved with education and prevention. ——警察应参与教育和预防工作。
- The police should be in close contact with schools. ——警方应与学校保持密切联系。
- They should focus on young people who have dropped out of school. ——重点关注辍学青少年。
- These teenagers may become involved with gangs. ——这些少年可能卷入帮派。

### 惩罚与监狱：Punishments / Prisons
- Fines are used as punishment for minor crimes. ——轻微犯罪用罚款惩罚。
- If the crime is more serious, prison is the most common punishment. ——重罪最常用的惩罚是监禁。
- Some criminals pose a threat to society. ——一些罪犯对社会构成威胁。
- They are put in prison to ensure the safety of other citizens. ——把他们关进监狱是为了保障其他公民的安全。

### 反方：Negatives of Prisons
- Criminals are put together. ——罪犯被关在一起。
- They make friends with other offenders. ——他们与其他罪犯交上朋友。
- Many prisoners re-offend when they are released. ——许多囚犯出狱后再次犯罪。
- A criminal record makes finding a job more difficult. ——犯罪记录让人更难找工作。

### 解决方向：Rehabilitation（改造）
- Another aim of prisons is rehabilitation. ——监狱的另一目的是改造。
- Prisoners receive education or vocational training. ——囚犯接受教育或职业培训。
- Prisoners should learn personal skills and specific job skills. ——囚犯应学会个人技能和特定职业技能。
- Punishment could make prisoners' behaviour worse. ——单纯惩罚可能让囚犯行为更糟。
- Rehabilitation aims to make them better citizens. ——改造旨在让他们成为更好的公民。
- Rehabilitated prisoners are less likely to re-offend. ——被改造好的囚犯更不易再犯。

### 死刑：Capital Punishment（正方）
- Supporters say that capital punishment deters crime. ——支持者说死刑能震慑犯罪。
- Fear of the death penalty stops people from committing offences. ——对死刑的恐惧阻止人们犯罪。
- The death penalty shows that crime is not tolerated. ——死刑表明社会绝不纵容犯罪。
- It is a form of revenge. ——（批评者说）它是一种复仇。
- The cost of imprisonment is avoided. ——省去了终身监禁的花费。
- The offender cannot pose a threat to others. ——罪犯无法再威胁他人。

### 反方：Against Capital Punishment
- Innocent people could be wrongly convicted and executed. ——无辜者可能被错判处死。
- Crime rates are not necessarily reduced. ——犯罪率未必因此下降。
- Many criminals do not think they will be caught. ——许多罪犯根本没想过会被抓。
- Capital punishment is not a good deterrent. ——死刑的威慑力并不强。
- Executing prisoners creates a violent culture and encourages revenge. ——处决囚犯营造暴力文化、助长复仇心态。
- We have no right to take another human life. ——我们无权剥夺他人的生命。

### 社区服务：Community Service（正方）
- Community service is a way to reform offenders. ——社区服务是改造罪犯的一种方式。
- It could be a solution to prison overcrowding. ——它可以解决监狱人满为患的问题。
- It avoids the cost of imprisonment. ——它省下监禁成本。
- It makes offenders useful in their local communities. ——它让罪犯为当地社区做有益的事。
- They are required to clean streets or talk to school groups. ——比如清扫街道或到学校做警示宣讲。
- Offenders repay their community. ——罪犯以此回馈社区。
- They avoid the negative influence that prison can have. ——他们避开了监狱的负面影响。

### 反方：Against Community Service
- Community service is not a sufficient punishment. ——社区服务的惩罚力度不够。
- Criminals should be locked up, away from their victims. ——罪犯应被关押，与受害者隔离。

### 媒体与犯罪：Crime in the Media
- Crime is one of the main subjects of most news programmes. ——犯罪是多数新闻节目的主打内容。
- The mass media focus on violent and sensational crimes. ——大众媒体聚焦暴力和耸人听闻的案件。
- This leads to fear of crime among the public. ——这导致公众对犯罪的恐慌。
- Sensational stories attract more viewers or readers. ——耸动的报道能吸引更多观众读者。
- The media report crime stories in order to increase their audience. ——媒体报罪案是为了提高收视率/发行量。

【常用论证角度】安全/威慑、改造成本 vs 监禁成本、再犯率、公平与生命权、媒体逐利。

- Education gives people knowledge and skills. ——教育给人知识和技能。

- The best universities employ teachers who are experts in their fields. ——顶尖大学聘请各领域专家任教。
- Qualifications gained abroad can open the door to better job opportunities. ——海外学历为更好的工作机会开门。

### 反方：Drawbacks of Studying Abroad

- Students have problems with paperwork such as visa applications. ——学生要应付签证申请等文书麻烦。
- The language barrier can be a problem. ——语言障碍是个问题。
- Students have to find accommodation and pay bills. ——学生得自己找住处、付账单。
- Studying in a foreign language is challenging. ——用外语学习很有挑战。
- Living alone in an unfamiliar culture can cause homesickness. ——独自生活在陌生文化中会想家。

### 教育科技：Technology in Education: Advantages
- Technology is a powerful tool to engage students. ——科技是吸引学生投入学习的有力工具。
- Technology can make lessons much more interesting. ——科技能让课堂有趣得多。
- Students can do research using online resources. ——学生可以用网络资源做研究。
- Students can study at their own pace. ——学生可以按自己的节奏学习。
- Adults can take distance learning courses. ——成年人可以上远程课程。
- Students can study whenever and wherever they want. ——学生想什么时候学、在哪学都行。
- Students also learn skills which are useful for their future jobs. ——学生还学到对未来工作有用的技能。
- For example, they learn to write reports using a word processor. ——比如学会用文字处理软件写报告。

### 反方：Technology in Education: Disadvantages
- People rely too much on computers. ——人们过度依赖电脑。
- Young learners do not become proficient in some basic skills. ——低龄学习者练不好一些基本功。
- They use word processors with spell-checkers. ——他们用的文字软件自带拼写检查。
- Their handwriting and spelling may suffer. ——他们的书写和拼写可能退化。
- People should be able to write a letter by hand. ——人应该会手写信件。
- Technology is no substitute for a real teacher. ——科技替代不了真正的老师。
- Learners need a structured course. ——学习者需要体系化课程。
- An experienced teacher knows what materials to choose. ——有经验的老师知道如何选材。
- Computers are expensive to maintain and can be unreliable. ——电脑维护昂贵且可能不可靠。

### 立场：Technology in Education: Opinion
- Institutions should supplement traditional teaching with the use of technology. ——学校应用科技补充传统教学。
- Technology is part of everyday life. ——科技已是日常生活的一部分。
- It can enhance a teacher's lessons. ——它能为老师的课堂增色。
- Students can use online resources to help with homework. ——学生可用网络资源辅助做作业。
- Students must still learn to write by hand. ——学生仍须练习手写。
- They should still use traditional sources of information such as books. ——仍应使用书籍等传统信息来源。

### 发展中国家教育：Education in Developing Countries: Problems
- Children often have to work from an early age. ——孩子往往从幼年起就要干活。
- There are no schools in many areas. ——许多地区没有学校。
- Families do not have access to books or computers. ——家庭没有书和电脑。
- Literacy rates are often low. ——识字率往往很低。
- People in developing countries need knowledge and skills. ——发展中国家的人需要知识与技能。
- Education is the key to improving the economy of these countries. ——教育是改善这些国家经济的关键。

### 解决方案：Education in Developing Countries: Solutions
- Developed countries could help developing nations by providing money. ——发达国家可以出资帮助发展中国家。
- They could invest in schools and technology. ——它们可以投资学校和科技。
- They could supply the funds to build schools and pay for teachers. ——可以提供资金建学校、付教师工资。
- Children need to have access to free schooling. ——孩子需要能上免费学校。
- Computer equipment could be donated. ——可以捐赠电脑设备。
- The Internet can expose students to a world of knowledge and information. ——互联网能让学生接触全世界的知识与信息。
- Governments should make education compulsory for all children. ——政府应实行全民义务教育。
- They should encourage parents to send their children to school. ——应鼓励家长送孩子上学。
- Governments of developed and developing countries must work together. ——发达国家与发展中国家政府必须合作。

### 高等教育：Higher Education: Advantages
- There are many benefits to going to university. ——上大学好处很多。
- A degree can open the door to better employment prospects. ——学位为更好的就业前景开门。
- Economies are becoming increasingly knowledge-based. ——经济正日益转向知识驱动。
- Most jobs require specific knowledge and skills. ——大多数工作需要专门知识与技能。
- Skilled workers are needed for sectors such as information technology and engineering. ——IT、工程等行业需要技术工人。
- Repetitive manual jobs are now done by machines. ——重复性体力活现在由机器完成。
- Many factories have moved to developing countries. ——许多工厂已迁往发展中国家。

### 反方：Higher Education: Disadvantages
- A healthy economy needs a wide range of workers. ——健康的经济需要各种类型的劳动者。
- Some manual workers will always be needed. ——体力劳动者永远会被需要。
- A university degree is not necessary for many service professions. ——许多服务行业并不需要大学学位。
- Practical training is more important in some industries. ——有些行业实践培训更重要。

- Discipline problems are avoided by home-schooling. ——在家上学可避免纪律问题。

- Children will miss out on the social experience that school offers. ——孩子会错过学校提供的社交经历。
- At school, children learn how to get on with each other. ——在学校孩子学会与人相处。
- Home-schooled children may lack social skills. ——在家上学的孩子可能缺乏社交技能。
- Schools offer a better overall educational experience. ——学校提供更完整的教育体验。

- Schools need to work closely with parents. ——学校需与家长紧密合作。
- Discipline could be lacking at home. ——家庭里可能本来就缺管教。
- Parents must support the school rules. ——家长必须支持校规。
- They should take responsibility for their children's behaviour. ——家长应对孩子的行为负责。

### 全球变暖：Global Warming
- Gases such as carbon dioxide trap heat from the sun. ——二氧化碳等气体锁住太阳热量。
- This causes global temperatures to rise. ——这导致全球气温上升。
- This process is known as the greenhouse effect. ——这一过程即温室效应。
- Human activity is a major factor in the rise of the greenhouse gases. ——人类活动是温室气体增加的主因。
- Factories and vehicles produce emissions and exhaust fumes. ——工厂和车辆排放废气。
- Many developing countries are becoming industrialised. ——许多发展中国家正在工业化。
- The number of cars on our streets is growing. ——街上汽车越来越多。
- Cheap air travel is allowing more people to fly. ——廉价航空让更多人坐飞机。

### 影响：Effects of Global Warming
- Global warming will have a significant impact on our planet. ——全球变暖将对地球产生重大影响。
- Rising temperatures will cause melting of the polar ice caps. ——气温上升导致极地冰盖融化。
- Sea levels will rise. ——海平面将上升。
- We can expect more extreme weather conditions. ——极端天气会更频繁。
- Flooding and droughts may become more common. ——洪涝和干旱可能更常见。

### 人类影响：Impact of Humans on the Environment
- The increasing world population is putting pressure on natural resources. ——增长的世界人口给自然资源施压。
- Fossil fuels like oil and gas are running out. ——石油天然气等化石燃料正在枯竭。
- We are destroying wildlife habitats. ——我们正在毁坏野生动物栖息地。
- We have cut down enormous areas of rainforest. ——我们砍伐了大片雨林。
- This has led to the extinction of many species of animals and plants. ——这导致许多动植物物种灭绝。

### 解决方案（政府）：Solutions to Environment Problems
- Governments could introduce laws to limit emissions from factories. ——政府可立法限制工厂排放。
- They should invest in renewable energy from solar, wind or water power. ——应投资太阳能、风能、水能等可再生能源。
- They could impose "green taxes" on drivers and airline companies. ——可对司机和航空公司征收"绿色税"。
- Government campaigns should promote recycling. ——政府宣传应推广回收。

### 解决方案（个人）
- Natural areas and wild animals should be protected. ——自然区域和野生动物应受保护。
- Individuals should also try to be greener. ——个人也应努力更环保。
- We should take fewer flights abroad for holidays. ——度假应少坐飞机。
- We should take public transport rather than driving. ——多坐公交少开车。
- We should choose products with less packaging. ——选择包装更少的产品。
- We should recycle as much as possible. ——尽可能回收利用。

### 垃圾问题：Waste / Rubbish
- The amount of waste we produce has increased. ——我们制造的垃圾越来越多。
- This problem is a result of our consumer culture. ——这一问题是消费文化的产物。
- Products are not made to last. ——产品做得不耐用。
- If something breaks, we throw it away and buy a new one. ——东西坏了就扔了买新的。
- Advertisers encourage us to buy the newest fashions. ——广告商鼓动我们买最新款。
- Packaging is an important part of selling. ——包装是销售的重要环节。
- Most foods are sold in non-biodegradable plastic packaging. ——多数食品用不可降解塑料包装出售。
- The amount of household waste is growing. ——家庭垃圾量在增长。
- This waste ends up in landfill sites. ——这些垃圾最终进了填埋场。

### 乱扔垃圾：Litter
- People do not think about the consequences of dropping rubbish. ——人们乱扔垃圾时不考虑后果。
- They assume that somebody is paid to clean the streets. ——他们以为总有人拿工资扫大街。
- Plastic packaging does not break down easily. ——塑料包装不易降解。
- Most of the litter seen on streets is fast food packaging. ——街上垃圾大多是快餐包装。

### 回收与其他解决办法：Recycling and Other Solutions
- Companies should make goods that last longer. ——企业应生产更耐用的商品。
- They should not use so much packaging. ——企业不应使用过多包装。
- Governments should be stricter about waste produced by companies. ——政府应对企业产生的垃圾更严格。
- They should put legal limits on packaging. ——应以法律限制包装。
- Consumers should avoid buying over-packaged products. ——消费者应避免购买过度包装的产品。
- We should recycle and reuse useful materials. ——我们应回收再利用有用材料。
- There are collection banks for glass, paper and plastic bottles. ——设有玻璃、纸张、塑料瓶回收点。
- Households can use several rubbish bins to separate waste. ——家庭可用多个垃圾桶分类。
- Recycling saves energy and raw materials. ——回收节省能源和原材料。

### 核能：Nuclear Power: Positives
- There are several benefits to building more nuclear power stations. ——多建核电站有若干好处。
- Fossil fuels like oil and gas are running out. ——油气等化石燃料正在枯竭。
- Nuclear power is a relatively sustainable energy source. ——核能是相对可持续的能源。
- It can be used to produce electricity without wasting natural resources. ——它发电不浪费自然资源。
- It could replace the use of natural resources like coal, oil or gas. ——它可替代煤、石油、天然气等资源。
- Nuclear power stations are cleaner than fossil fuel power stations. ——核电站比火电厂清洁。
- They could help to reduce carbon emissions that cause global warming. ——它们有助于减少导致全球变暖的碳排放。
- The risks of accidents are being reduced. ——事故风险正在降低。

### 反方：Nuclear Power: Negatives
- Opponents of nuclear power worry about the safety of power stations. ——反核者担心核电站安全。
- The building of new nuclear power stations is unpopular. ——新建核电站不得人心。
- Nobody wants to live near one. ——没人想住在核电站旁边。
- Nuclear waste disposal is a significant problem. ——核废料处理是大难题。
- There is currently no way to decontaminate radioactive material. ——目前没有办法清除放射性污染。
- People worry that terrorists could steal radioactive materials. ——人们担心恐怖分子窃取放射性材料。
- It is safer to produce energy from solar, wind or water power. ——用太阳能、风能、水能发电更安全。

- Families in many countries are not as large as they used to be. ——许多国家的家庭不再像过去那么大。

### 双职工家庭：Working Parents (also see "Gender" topic)
- Children and their parents seem to be less close nowadays. ——如今孩子与父母似乎不那么亲密了。
- Parents spend less time with their children. ——父母陪孩子的时间变少。
- Women traditionally stayed at home to cook, clean and look after children. ——传统上女性在家做饭、打扫、照顾孩子。

- Children spend more time with friends or surfing the Internet. ——孩子花更多时间跟朋友玩或上网。

- Many parents have no idea how their children spend their time. ——许多父母完全不知道孩子的时间花在哪。

- Parents should be more involved with their children's upbringing. ——父母应更多参与孩子的养育。
- Young people need positive role models. ——年轻人需要正面的榜样。

### 离婚：Divorce
- In the past, divorce was unacceptable. ——过去离婚不被接受。
- It was considered to be embarrassing for a family. ——离婚被视为家庭的耻辱。
- People stayed together for religious or family reasons. ——人们因宗教或家族原因维持婚姻。
- Divorce is more socially acceptable nowadays. ——如今离婚更被社会接受。
- It has become much more common. ——离婚变得普遍得多。
- Divorce can be extremely stressful. ——离婚可能造成极大压力。
- Lone parents may face financial difficulties. ——单亲家长可能面临经济困难。
- Many single parents have to rely on benefits paid by the state. ——许多单亲家长不得不依靠国家救济。
- Divorce can have a negative effect on children. ——离婚可能对孩子产生负面影响。
- Children from single-parent families are more likely to get lower grades or drop out of school. ——单亲家庭的孩子更可能成绩差或辍学。
- The rise in divorce rates may be connected to some social problems. ——离婚率上升可能与某些社会问题相关。

### 养老：Care for Old People
- Caring for elderly people was traditionally the responsibility of families. ——照顾老人传统上是家庭的责任。
- Adults had to look after their elderly parents. ——成年人必须照顾年迈的父母。
- A woman's job was to stay at home taking care of her family. ——女性的职责是留在家中照顾家人。
- Nowadays, fewer elderly people are looked after by their relatives. ——如今由亲属照料的老人越来越少。
- Residential homes provide care for large numbers of elderly people. ——养老院为大量老人提供照护。
- Some families are unable to look after elderly relatives. ——有些家庭无力照顾年迈的亲人。
- Families tend to be smaller these days, and women often have full-time jobs. ——如今家庭变小，女性常有全职工作。
- Care homes provide a professional service for senior citizens. ——养老院为老人提供专业服务。
- Nurses are better trained than family members. ——护士比家庭成员受训更专业。

### 立场：Care for Old People: Opinion
- The best form of care for the elderly depends on the family situation. ——最好的养老方式取决于家庭情况。
- It depends on whether family members have the time and resources. ——取决于家人是否有时间和资源。
- We all have a responsibility towards the older people in our society. ——我们对社会中的老人都负有责任。
- Governments should invest money in facilities and training for care workers. ——政府应投资养老设施并培训护理人员。

### 研究家族史（官网补充材料 family-history，真题原文摘录）
> **真题问法**："In some parts of the world it is becoming popular to research the history of one's own family. Why might people want to do this? Is it a positive or negative development?"
> （在世界一些地方，研究自己的家族史正在流行。人们为什么想这么做？这是积极还是消极的发展？）
> Simon 提醒：这是 "2-part question"——不是写家族史利弊的讨论文，而是先解释原因、再给立场。

**人们为什么研究家族史（Why）：**
- People want to know more about themselves and their roots, where they come from, whether their ancestors had similar personality traits. ——人们想更了解自己与自己的"根"：来自哪里、祖先是否有相似的性格。
- The Internet makes it easier to do this research, we hear about others who have done it, and there are even advertisements to encourage us. ——互联网让研究更容易，我们听说别人在做，甚至有广告鼓励我们尝试。
- People do it out of curiosity; we might secretly hope that we have a famous or wealthy ancestor. ——出于好奇：我们可能暗自希望自己有名人或富豪祖先。

**积极面（Positive，Simon 的立场）：**
- It is a fascinating hobby and a nice way to spend free time. ——这是迷人的爱好，是打发闲暇的好方式。
- It is a good talking point with family and friends, e.g. if we discover a particularly interesting ancestor. ——是亲友间的好谈资，比如发现一位特别有意思的祖先。
- It is a good way to teach children about history and how earlier generations lived. ——是教孩子历史、让后代了解先辈生活的好方式。
- We may even find "long-lost" family members and distant relatives that we might have the chance to meet. ——甚至可能找到"失散多年"的家人、有机会见面的远亲。
- Simon 建议积累的话题词汇：family tree（家谱）、ancestors（祖先）、genealogy（宗谱学）、tracing family history（追溯家族史）、a person's roots（一个人的根）。

【常用论证角度】时间（陪伴）、金钱（养家/福利）、心理（亲密感、榜样、好奇）、社会影响（青少年犯罪）、责任主体（父母/政府）。

## 9. Genetic Engineering 基因工程

【问法方向】
- 基因工程的利与弊？(Positives / Negatives)
- 转基因食品的利与弊？(GM Foods)

### 正方：Positives of Genetic Engineering
- Genetic engineering is the practice of manipulating the genes of an organism. ——基因工程是操控生物基因的技术。
- It is used to produce crops that are more resistant to insects and diseases. ——它被用于培育更抗虫抗病的作物。
- Some genetically modified crops grow more quickly. ——一些转基因作物生长更快。
- Some drugs and vaccines are produced by genetic engineering. ——一些药物和疫苗由基因工程生产。
- It may become possible to change humans' genetic characteristics. ——改变人类基因特征或将成为可能。
- Scientists may use genetic engineering to cure diseases. ——科学家或可用基因工程治病。
- Inherited illnesses would no longer exist. ——遗传病将不复存在。
- Genes could be changed before a baby is born. ——基因可在婴儿出生前被修改。
- It could also be possible to clone human organs. ——克隆人体器官也将成为可能。
- We could all have replacement body parts. ——我们都可能拥有替换的器官。
- Humans could live longer, healthier lives. ——人类可以活得更长、更健康。

### 反方：Negatives of Genetic Engineering
- There are ethical concerns about human genetic engineering. ——人类基因工程存在伦理隐忧。
- Parents might want to choose their children's characteristics. ——父母可能想挑选孩子的特征。
- This would be unnatural. ——这将违背自然。
- It would be unacceptable in most religions. ——大多数宗教都无法接受。
- Soldiers could be cloned from the genes of the strongest people. ——可能用最强壮者的基因克隆士兵。
- Clones might be used like robots to do certain jobs. ——克隆人可能被当作机器人干粗活。
- Clones might even be developed just for organ replacements. ——克隆人甚至可能只为提供器官而被制造。
- Society and human evolution would change completely. ——社会和人类进化将彻底改变。
- Currently, human genetic engineering is prohibited. ——目前人类基因工程是被禁止的。

### 转基因食品：Genetically-Modified (GM) Foods: Advantages
- Farmers can produce crops that grow bigger and faster. ——农民能种出更大更快的作物。
- Some GM crops are more resistant to disease or insects. ——一些转基因作物更抗病抗虫。
- This could be important for food production in developing countries. ——这对发展中国家的粮食生产意义重大。
- Faster growing cereals, fruit and vegetables will mean more profit. ——更快的谷物果蔬生长意味着更多利润。
- GM foods can be modified to look perfect. ——转基因食品可以被改得外观完美。
- They may be more attractive to customers. ——它们可能对顾客更有吸引力。

### 反方：Genetically-Modified (GM) Foods: Disadvantages
- There may be risks involved in the genetic engineering of foods. ——食物基因工程可能存在风险。
- GM crops might change whole ecosystems. ——转基因作物可能改变整个生态系统。
- Food chains could be broken if crops are resistant to predators. ——若作物抗天敌，食物链可能断裂。
- Organic foods are produced without chemicals or genetic modification. ——有机食品不使用化学品、不经基因改造。
- Organic farming may be slower and more expensive. ——有机农业可能更慢、更贵。
- However, the environment is not damaged by fertilisers or pesticides. ——但环境不受化肥农药损害。

【常用论证角度】健康、环境（生态系统）、经济（产量/利润）、伦理与宗教、长远风险 vs 短期收益。

## 10. Global Issues 全球议题

【问法方向】
- 发展中国家面临什么问题？发达国家该怎么帮？(Problems / How to Help)
- 移民的利与弊、该怎么管理？(Immigration)
- 全球化的利与弊、未来走向？(Globalisation)

### 发展中国家的问题：Problems in Developing Countries
- Developing countries face a range of problems. ——发展中国家面临一系列问题。
- Standards of healthcare and education are low. ——医疗和教育水平低。
- Life expectancy is usually lower than in developed countries. ——预期寿命通常低于发达国家。
- There is a lack of infrastructure, employment and good quality housing. ——缺乏基础设施、就业和优质住房。
- Many people are forced to live in poverty. ——许多人被迫生活在贫困中。
- Food, drinking water and basic medicines are in short supply. ——食物、饮用水和基本药品短缺。

### 帮助发展中国家：How to Help Developing Countries
- The best form of help for developing countries is development aid. ——对发展中国家最好的帮助是发展援助。
- Richer nations can help by investing in long-term projects. ——富国可通过投资长期项目提供帮助。
- Governments and charities can help by building new houses and schools. ——政府和慈善机构可援建住房和学校。
- Globalisation may also help developing countries. ——全球化也可能帮助发展中国家。
- Multi-national companies are creating jobs in developing countries. ——跨国公司正在发展中国家创造就业。
- On the other hand, many people emigrate to find work in richer countries. ——另一方面，许多人移居富国打工。
- They often send money back home to their families. ——他们常往家里寄钱。
- This money helps to improve the standard of living in developing countries. ——这些钱帮助提高发展中国家的生活水平。

### 移民背景：Immigration
- Some people move to another country in search of a better life. ——一些人为追求更好的生活移居他国。
- Many immigrants come from less developed countries. ——许多移民来自欠发达国家。
- Richer, industrialised countries may offer opportunities for employment. ——富裕工业化国家能提供就业机会。
- Free healthcare and schooling are available in some developed countries. ——一些发达国家提供免费医疗和教育。
- Other people migrate to a foreign country to improve their academic qualifications. ——也有人出国是为了提升学历。

### 正方：Positives of Immigration and Multi-Cultural Societies
- From an economic perspective, immigration can be extremely positive. ——从经济角度看，移民极为正面。
- Many immigrants have skills that are needed in the country they move to. ——许多移民拥有移入国所需的技能。
- Immigrants who find work contribute to the economy of their new country. ——找到工作的移民为新国家经济做贡献。
- Many immigrants send money home to help family members. ——许多移民寄钱回家接济家人。
- Immigration also creates cultural diversity. ——移民还造就文化多样性。
- People of many different nationalities learn to live together. ——不同国籍的人学会共同生活。
- This can help people to become more open-minded and tolerant. ——这能让人更开放、更宽容。

### 反方：Negatives of Immigration
- Some people believe that immigrants take jobs that should go to local people. ——有人认为移民抢走了本该属于本地人的工作。
- Some immigrant workers work longer hours for less money. ——一些移民工工时更长、工资更低。
- Companies might pay lower salaries to immigrant workers. ——公司可能给移民工开更低工资。
- Unemployment rates could rise if there are too many immigrants. ——移民太多可能导致失业率上升。

### 立场：Opinions about Immigration
- Immigration can help the economy of a country. ——移民能帮助国家经济。
- It can create multi-cultural societies. ——它能造就多元文化社会。
- However, immigration needs to be controlled. ——但移民需要受到管控。
- In many countries, immigrants need visas or work permits. ——在许多国家，移民需要签证或工作许可。
- Governments should stop companies from exploiting immigrant workers. ——政府应制止企业剥削移民工。
- Foreign and local workers should have the right to equal pay and conditions. ——外籍与本地工人应享有同工同酬、同等待遇的权利。

### 正方：Positives of Globalisation
- Business is becoming increasingly international. ——商业正日益国际化。
- Multi-national companies do business across the world. ——跨国公司在全球经营。
- Companies like MacDonald's can be seen on high streets in most cities. ——麦当劳这样的品牌出现在大多数城市的商业街。
- Goods are produced in one country and sold in many others. ——商品在一国生产、销往多国。
- A global economy means free trade between countries. ——全球经济意味着国家间自由贸易。
- This can strengthen political relationships. ——这能巩固政治关系。
- Globalisation can also create opportunities for employment. ——全球化也能创造就业机会。
- It encourages investment in less developed countries. ——它鼓励对欠发达国家的投资。
- It could reduce poverty in the developing world. ——它可能减少发展中国家的贫困。

### 反方：Negatives of Globalisation
- Globalisation is not always beneficial to everyone. ——全球化并不总对人人有利。
- Companies can move to countries where labour is cheap. ——公司可以迁往劳动力廉价的国家。
- This creates redundancies, or job losses. ——这造成裁员和失业。
- Employees cannot be confident that they have stable jobs. ——雇员无法确信自己工作稳定。
- Companies sometimes exploit their employees in developing countries. ——公司有时剥削发展中国家的员工。
- Global trade creates more waste and pollution. ——全球贸易制造更多垃圾和污染。

### 未来：The Future of Globalisation
- There should be global regulations for salaries and working conditions. ——应就工资和工作条件制定全球规则。
- Governments should impose laws to protect the environment. ——各国政府应立法保护环境。

【常用论证角度】经济（就业/投资/汇款）、文化多样性 vs 本土冲击、公平（同工同酬/剥削）、环境、全球治理。

- Free education and healthcare may be provided by the state. ——国家可以提供免费教育和医疗。
- Some governments control public transport systems and even TV channels. ——一些政府掌控公共交通系统甚至电视频道。
- In other countries, these services are provided by private companies. ——在另一些国家，这些服务由私营公司提供。
- Some people believe that competition between private companies is good. ——有人认为私营企业间的竞争是好事。
- It helps to improve quality while bringing prices down. ——竞争有助于提升质量、降低价格。
- Other people think that essential services should be free. ——另一些人认为基本服务应当免费。
- Governments should pay for them. ——政府应当买单。

### 媒体审查：Censorship: Opinion
- Governments can censor what the public sees or reads in the media. ——政府可以审查公众在媒体上看什么、读什么。
- To a certain extent censorship is necessary. ——一定程度上的审查是必要的。
- We should use censorship to protect children from violent images. ——应利用审查保护儿童远离暴力画面。
- Some computer games involve killing people or committing crimes. ——一些电脑游戏包含杀人或犯罪情节。
- The Internet also needs to be controlled. ——互联网也需要管制。
- Many websites show pornography and violence. ——许多网站含有色情和暴力内容。
- There should be age limits for websites and computer games. ——网站和电脑游戏应有年龄限制。
- Parents need to take responsibility for checking what their children watch. ——家长有责任检查孩子看的内容。
- It is impossible for governments to control everything we see. ——政府不可能管住我们看到的一切。

### 公共场所监控：Video Cameras in Public Places（书中观点）
- The use of CCTV is becoming widespread. ——闭路电视监控正变得无处不在。
- Video cameras have been installed in many public places. ——许多公共场所安装了摄像头。
- They are supposed to protect us and deter criminals. ——它们本应保护我们、震慑罪犯。
- Many people think that this surveillance violates our privacy. ——很多人认为这种监控侵犯隐私。
- The authorities could build databases with our pictures and identities. ——当局可能建立包含我们影像和身份的数据库。
- We should not be treated like criminals. ——我们不应像罪犯一样被对待。

- Governments will probably introduce a digital identification card system. ——政府可能推出数字身份证系统。

- Digital bank cards could contain fingerprint information. ——数字银行卡可包含指纹信息。
- It would be very difficult for criminals to use a stolen card. ——罪犯很难使用偷来的卡。
- It would be easier for police to identify people and catch criminals. ——警察更容易识别身份、抓捕罪犯。

- Governments could store all our personal and medical information. ——政府可能存储我们所有个人和医疗信息。
- This information could be used by insurance companies. ——这些信息可能被保险公司利用。
- Employers could check our health records. ——雇主可能查看我们的健康档案。

- They should have access to the same jobs as other citizens. ——他们应能获得与其他公民相同的工作。
- Discrimination against disabled people is illegal in many countries. ——在许多国家歧视残障人士是违法的。
- Ramps and lifts for wheelchairs should be installed in public buildings. ——公共建筑应安装轮椅坡道和电梯。
- Support teachers can be employed to help children with learning difficulties. ——可聘请辅导教师帮助有学习障碍的儿童。

## 12. Guns and Weapons 枪支与武器

【问法方向】
- 普通人该不该有权持枪？(Why guns should be legal / illegal)
- 警察该不该配枪？(Why police should / should not use guns)
- 军火贸易的利与弊？(Arms Trade)
- 核武器怎么看？该不该有军队？(Nuclear Weapons / Armed Forces)

### 持枪合法的理由：Why guns should be legal
- In some countries, people are allowed to own firearms. ——在一些国家人们可以拥有枪支。
- Individuals have the right to protect themselves. ——个人有自卫的权利。
- People can use guns in self defence. ——人们可以用枪自卫。
- This deters criminals. ——这能震慑罪犯。

### 反方：Why gun ownership should be illegal
- There is a risk of accidents with guns. ——枪支有事故风险。
- The number of violent crimes increases when guns are available. ——枪支可得时暴力犯罪增加。
- Criminals may be armed. ——罪犯可能持枪。
- The police then need to use guns. ——警察于是也不得不用枪。
- Suicide rates have been shown to rise when guns are available. ——研究表明枪支可得时自杀率上升。
- Guns create violent societies with high murder rates. ——枪支造就凶杀率高的暴力社会。

### 警察配枪的理由：Why police should use guns
- Many criminals use weapons. ——许多罪犯使用武器。
- The threat of a gun can deter criminals. ——枪的威慑力可吓退罪犯。
- Police officers can force a criminal to surrender. ——警察可以逼迫罪犯投降。
- It is easier to arrest someone and avoid physical violence. ——更容易实施抓捕、避免肢体冲突。
- The police may shoot violent criminals in self defence. ——警察可出于自卫击毙暴力罪犯。
- They can protect the public. ——他们能保护公众。
- They can shoot an escaping criminal who poses a serious danger to the public. ——可击毙对公众构成严重危险的逃犯。

### 反方：Why police should not carry guns
- There is a risk of accidents and mistakes. ——存在事故与误判风险。
- The police might shoot an unarmed criminal or an innocent person. ——警察可能误射手无寸铁者或无辜者。
- Accidents can happen in public places. ——事故可能发生在公共场所。
- There are several alternatives to guns (e.g. tear gas, sprays and electric shock weapons). ——枪有多种替代品（催泪瓦斯、喷雾、电击武器）。
- Only special police units should use guns. ——只有特别警队才应配枪。

### 军火贸易：Arms Trade: Positives
- The export of arms, or weapons, is an extremely controversial issue. ——武器出口是极具争议的议题。
- Governments of rich, industrialised countries sell arms to each other. ——富裕工业国相互出售武器。
- This industry creates jobs and wealth. ——该产业创造就业和财富。
- The trade of weapons may improve relationships between governments. ——武器贸易或可改善政府间关系。

### 反方：Arms Trade: Negatives
- Weapons may be used in conflicts and wars. ——武器可能被用于冲突和战争。
- The supply of arms could be responsible for deaths. ——武器供应可能造成死亡。
- Governments are promoting war in order to make a profit. ——政府为牟利而鼓动战争。
- Rich countries can influence the politics of other nations. ——富国可以操纵他国政治。

### 核武器：Nuclear Weapons
- Nuclear weapons are capable of destroying whole cities. ——核武器能摧毁整座城市。
- A nuclear war between two countries would destroy both countries. ——两国核战会同归于尽。
- Nuclear weapons are used as a deterrent. ——核武器被用作威慑。
- They prevent wars from starting. ——它们阻止战争爆发。

### 立场：Nuclear Weapons: Opinion
- Nuclear weapons should be prohibited. ——核武器应被禁止。
- Governments should limit the production of nuclear weapons. ——政府应限制核武器生产。
- There is a danger of nuclear weapons being obtained by terrorists. ——核武器有落入恐怖分子之手的危险。
- Nuclear weapons cannot be used against terrorist organisations. ——核武器对恐怖组织毫无用处。

### 军队：Armed Forces: Positives
- Armed forces provide security and protection. ——军队提供安全保障。
- They deter military attack by another country. ——他们威慑他国的军事进攻。
- They can also be used to maintain peace within countries. ——军队也可用于维护国内和平。
- They can be used to give the police extra support. ——可为警察提供额外支持。
- Soldiers are also used to help in emergency situations, such as after a natural disaster. ——军人还参与应急救援，如自然灾害之后。

### 反方：Armed Forces: Negatives
- Armies require a lot of funding from governments. ——军队需要政府大量拨款。
- Too much money is spent on weapons and military technology. ——太多钱花在武器和军事科技上。
- This money could be spent on schools, hospitals and other public services. ——这些钱本可以花在学校、医院和其他公共服务上。

【常用论证角度】安全/威慑 vs 事故风险、生命权、金钱的机会成本（军费 vs 民生）、就业与财富、政治影响。

## 13. Health 健康

【问法方向】
- 现代人饮食为什么不健康？(Diet)
- 运动的重要性、现代人为什么缺运动？(Exercise)
- 政府在公众健康中该做什么？(Government's Role)
- 公立医疗 vs 私立医疗？(State Health Systems / Private Healthcare)
- 替代医学（针灸草药）可信吗？(Alternative Medicine)
- 现代生活的压力与解法？(Stress)

### 饮食：Diet
- The human body requires a balanced diet. ——人体需要均衡饮食。
- An unhealthy diet can cause various health problems. ——不健康饮食引发多种健康问题。
- Obesity, diabetes and heart disease are on the increase. ——肥胖、糖尿病和心脏病正在增多。
- Many people nowadays rely on fast food or pre-prepared meals. ——如今许多人依赖快餐和预加工食品。
- These foods often contain too much fat, salt and sugar. ——这些食品往往含过多脂肪、盐和糖。
- They are cheap to buy and very easy to prepare. ——它们便宜又省事。
- Many young people have grown up on a diet of convenience foods. ——许多年轻人是吃便利食品长大的。
- Populations in developed countries are increasingly overweight. ——发达国家人口越来越超重。

### 运动：Exercise
- Regular exercise is essential in maintaining a healthy body. ——规律运动是保持健康的关键。
- Exercise burns calories and helps to build healthy bones and muscles. ——运动燃烧热量、强健骨骼肌肉。
- Doctors advise exercising at least three times a week for 20 minutes. ——医生建议每周至少运动三次、每次20分钟。
- Most people nowadays lead a sedentary lifestyle. ——如今多数人久坐少动。
- We tend to walk less and do desk jobs. ——我们走路更少、工作伏案。
- Most adults relax by watching television. ——多数成人靠看电视放松。
- Children play video games rather than doing outdoor sports. ——孩子玩电子游戏而不做户外运动。
- In the past, people were more active in their jobs and at home. ——过去人们在工作生活中活动更多。

### 政府的角色：Government's Role
- Governments have a significant role to play in reducing obesity. ——政府在减少肥胖上大有可为。
- More and more people, including young children, are seriously overweight. ——越来越多的人（包括幼儿）严重超重。
- They are at risk of heart disease and diabetes. ——他们面临心脏病和糖尿病风险。
- This situation will increase the burden on hospitals and taxpayers. ——这种情况将加重医院和纳税人的负担。
- Hospitals rely on the government for money and resources. ——医院靠政府提供资金和资源。
- Governments should promote a healthy diet and regular exercise. ——政府应提倡健康饮食和规律运动。
- There should be more time for sport on school timetables. ——学校课程表应安排更多体育时间。
- Unhealthy junk food should be banned from school menus. ——学校菜单应禁掉不健康的垃圾食品。
- People need information about what foods contain. ——人们需要了解食品成分。
- Food packaging must show the food's nutritional content. ——食品包装必须标注营养成分。
- The British Government recommends eating five portions of fruit and vegetables per day. ——英国政府建议每天吃五份果蔬。

### 公立医疗：State Health Systems: Advantages
- Good healthcare should be available to everyone for free. ——好的医疗应人人免费享有。
- State healthcare is paid by the government using money from taxes. ——公立医疗由政府用税款支付。
- Everyone has access to the same quality of care and treatment. ——人人享有同等质量的医疗。
- Private healthcare is unfair because only wealthy people can afford it. ——私立医疗不公平，因为只有富人负担得起。
- The National Health Service in the UK provides free healthcare for every resident. ——英国国民医疗服务体系（NHS）为每个居民提供免费医疗。

### 私立医疗：Private Healthcare: Advantages
- State hospitals are often very large and difficult to run. ——公立医院往往庞大难管。
- Private hospitals have shorter waiting lists for operations and appointments. ——私立医院手术和门诊等待时间短。
- Patients can benefit from faster treatment. ——病人能更快得到治疗。
- Many people prefer to pay for a more personal service. ——许多人愿意花钱买更贴心的服务。
- Patients have their own room and more comfortable facilities. ——病人有单间和更舒适的设施。

### 替代医学：Alternative Medicine: Positives
- People are increasingly using alternative medicines to treat illnesses. ——越来越多人用替代医学治病。
- For example, acupuncture can be used to treat backache. ——例如针灸可用于治背痛。
- Herbal medicines can be used to treat allergies or viruses. ——草药可用于治疗过敏或病毒性疾病。
- Many patients report positive experiences with these treatments. ——许多病人反映这些疗法有效。
- Some traditional cures have been used for hundreds of years. ——一些传统疗法已沿用了数百年。

### 反方：Alternative Medicine: Negatives
- Many alternative medicines have not been tested scientifically. ——许多替代药物未经科学检验。
- They may have no beneficial effects at all. ——它们可能毫无疗效。
- They may cause unknown side effects. ——它们可能引起未知的副作用。
- People should trust the opinions of qualified doctors. ——人们应听从有资质医生的意见。
- An illness could get worse without treatment from a doctor. ——不经医生治疗，病情可能恶化。

### 压力：Stress
- Modern lifestyles are increasingly stressful. ——现代生活方式压力越来越大。
- People work long hours with strict deadlines. ——人们工时长、截止期限严。
- Our busy lifestyles mean we have less time to relax. ——忙碌的生活意味着放松时间更少。
- Unemployment is a major cause of stress. ——失业是压力的重要来源。
- Children may be affected by their parents' relationship problems. ——孩子可能受父母感情问题影响。
- Tests and exams can also cause stress. ——考试测验也会带来压力。

### 减压：How to Reduce Stress
- Stress can be reduced by taking regular exercise and eating a healthy diet. ——规律运动和健康饮食能减压。
- It is also important to get sufficient sleep and make leisure time a priority. ——充足睡眠、把休闲放在优先位置也很重要。
- People should work less overtime and take regular holidays. ——少加班、定期休假。
- Schools have started to employ psychologists. ——学校开始聘请心理老师。
- They can offer emotional support to students. ——他们能给学生情绪支持。
- They can help students to cope with exam stress. ——他们帮学生应对考试压力。

【常用论证角度】健康本身、政府职责（宣传/立法/菜单/课表）、公平（免费医疗）vs 效率（私立更快）、花钱买服务、心理（压力）、信息透明（营养标签）。

## 14. Housing and Architecture 住房与建筑

【问法方向】
- 政府该不该提供保障房/廉租房？(State / Council Housing)
- 该不该保护老建筑？(Old Buildings)
- 现代建筑该怎么设计？(Modern / Green Buildings)

### 保障房：State / Council Housing
- In some countries the government provides state or council housing. ——一些国家由政府提供保障房。
- This helps people who cannot afford to buy their own house. ——这帮助了买不起房的人。
- It can be argued that state housing creates dependence on the government. ——有人认为保障房造成对政府的依赖。
- People should not rely on the government to look after them. ——人们不应指望政府来照顾自己。
- People have no incentive to earn money and buy their own home. ——人们就没有挣钱买房的动力了。
- Council properties are often made with cheap, poor-quality materials. ——保障房往往用料便宜、质量差。

### 老建筑：Old Buildings
- Historic buildings are part of a country's heritage. ——历史建筑是国家遗产的一部分。
- They should be protected. ——它们应受保护。
- Old buildings are often considered to be works of art. ——老建筑常被视为艺术品。
- They give character to cities and attract tourists. ——它们赋予城市个性、吸引游客。
- They show us how people lived in the past. ——它们展示过去人们的生活方式。
- We identify countries by architectural symbols like the pyramids in Egypt. ——我们通过建筑符号识别国家，如埃及金字塔。
- Governments should spend money on looking after historic buildings. ——政府应花钱维护历史建筑。
- They need regular repairs and maintenance. ——老建筑需要定期修缮。
- New buildings should be designed to complement them. ——新建筑设计应与老建筑相得益彰。

### 现代绿色建筑：Modern / Green Buildings
- Modern buildings should be designed to be environmentally friendly. ——现代建筑应设计得环保。
- They should use less energy and produce less waste. ——它们应更节能、更少产生垃圾。
- Modern insulation can make houses more energy-efficient. ——现代保温材料能让房屋更节能。
- Solar and wind power can be used to generate electricity. ——可用太阳能和风能发电。
- Rainwater and waste water can be recycled and used to flush toilets. ——雨水和废水可回收用于冲厕。
- Modern glass buildings take advantage of natural light. ——现代玻璃建筑善用自然光。

【常用论证角度】政府责任 vs 个人自立（依赖问题）、文化传承与旅游、环境与能效、金钱成本。

## 15. Language 语言

- 语言单一化/小语种消亡的坏处？另一种"世界语"可行吗？(Negatives)

### 正方：English as an International Language
- English is widely used around the world. ——英语在全世界广泛使用。
- It is becoming a global second language. ——它正在成为全球性第二语言。

- Jeans and T-shirts are now worn throughout the world. ——牛仔裤和T恤已风靡全球。
- Historic buildings took skilled craftsmen years to build. ——历史建筑曾需要工匠们建上多年。
- Modern concrete, steel and glass buildings are built in only weeks or months. ——现代混凝土钢筋玻璃建筑数周数月即可建成。
- There are fewer people who can create hand-made goods. ——会做手工制品的人越来越少。

### 传统习俗：Traditional Customs
- Traditional customs are still important during weddings and religious festivals. ——传统习俗在婚礼和宗教节日中依然重要。
- People wear traditional costumes and eat special foods. ——人们穿传统服饰、吃特色食物。
- It is important to maintain our different cultural identities. ——保持各自不同的文化身份很重要。
- We should celebrate festivals, teach traditional skills and protect historic places. ——我们应庆祝节日、传授传统技艺、保护历史遗迹。

【常用论证角度】效率与廉价 vs 文化身份、就业（手艺人）、心理（成就感）、保护措施（教育/政府）。

## 22. Transport 交通

【问法方向】
- 交通拥堵的原因？(Traffic Problems)
- 怎么解决交通问题？(Traffic Solutions)
- 公共交通的利与弊？(Public Transport)
- 如何提升道路安全？(Road Safety)

### 拥堵原因：Traffic Problems
- Traffic congestion is caused by commuters travelling to work. ——拥堵由通勤上班族造成。
- Most people live in the suburbs outside city centres. ——多数人住在市中心外的郊区。
- Commuters tend to travel at the same time of day. ——通勤者往往在同一时段出行。
- They tend to travel alone. ——他们往往独自驾车。
- Cars and road space are not used efficiently. ——汽车和道路空间利用低效。
- This causes traffic jams during the rush hour. ——这导致高峰期堵车。

### 解决方案：Traffic Solutions
- In order to reduce traffic we should change our working habits. ——要减少车流应改变工作方式。
- The Internet can now be used to connect people. ——如今互联网可以把人连接起来。
- More people could work from home. ——更多人可以在家办公。
- Meetings can be held as video conferences. ——会议可以视频召开。
- Workers could be given flexible timetables. ——可以实行弹性工作制。
- Another solution would be to tax drivers. ——另一个办法是对司机征税。
- Workers should share their cars and travel together. ——上班族应拼车出行。
- In London, for example, there is a congestion charge. ——比如伦敦有拥堵费。
- This helps to raise money for better public transport. ——这为改善公共交通筹集了资金。
- Public transport needs to be reliable and efficient. ——公共交通必须可靠、高效。

### 公共交通：Positives of Public Transport
- We need to reduce our dependence on cars. ——我们需要减少对汽车的依赖。
- Parking a car can be extremely difficult in big cities. ——大城市停车极难。
- Well-designed transport systems are comfortable and convenient. ——设计良好的公交系统舒适便捷。
- Modern public transport can be fast and cheap. ——现代公交可以又快又便宜。
- Public transport can help to reduce pollution in cities. ——公交有助于减少城市污染。
- Investment in buses and trains will ease traffic congestion. ——投资公交火车能缓解拥堵。
- Buses can be given special lanes to avoid traffic. ——公交车可设专用道避开拥堵。

### 反方：Negatives of Public Transport
- Public transport is often slow and unreliable. ——公交常常又慢又不准点。
- Metro systems and trains are often dirty and crowded. ——地铁火车常常又脏又挤。
- People feel like "sardines in a can". ——人们感觉像"罐头里的沙丁鱼"。
- Cars are much more comfortable. ——汽车舒服得多。

### 道路安全：Road Safety
- Driving while tired or drunk is extremely dangerous. ——疲劳驾驶和酒驾极其危险。
- Mobile phones can be a dangerous distraction for drivers. ——手机对司机是危险的干扰。
- They draw the driver's attention away from the road. ——它们让司机分心离开路面。
- The use of phones while driving has been banned in many countries. ——许多国家已禁止开车用手机。
- Punishments are becoming stricter. ——处罚越来越严。
- Television campaigns are used to remind people to drive safely. ——电视宣传提醒人们安全驾驶。
- Speed cameras have become more common. ——测速摄像头越来越普遍。
- Speed bumps are another form of traffic calming. ——减速带是另一种降速手段。
- Many streets are designed with the aim of slowing traffic down. ——许多街道的设计以减速为目标。

【常用论证角度】效率（道路空间利用）、时间（堵车）、环境（污染）、金钱（税费/投资）、科技方案（远程办公）、执法与宣传。

## 23. Water 水

【问法方向】
- 清洁饮用水为什么重要？(Importance of Clean Water)
- 水资源短缺与供应问题？(Water Supply)
- 水与政治的关系？(Water and Politics)
- 水该不该免费？(Water should be free / not free)
- 瓶装水怎么看？(Bottled Water)

### 清洁水的重要性：Importance of Clean Water
- Water is an essential natural resource. ——水是必不可少的自然资源。
- Humans need access to clean, safe drinking water in order to live. ——人要活命就必须有干净安全的饮用水。
- Poor water quality is a major cause of disease and death in some countries. ——在有些国家，水质差是致病致死的主因。
- Water usually needs to be treated before we can drink it. ——水通常要先处理才能饮用。
- Developing countries often lack the means to treat and supply water to citizens. ——发展中国家往往缺乏处理和供水的能力。
- Developed countries tend to have much better sanitation. ——发达国家卫生条件好得多。
- Citizens have access to clean tap water. ——市民能用到干净的自来水。
- Drinking water is not contaminated by sewage or waste water. ——饮用水没有被污水废水污染。
- The supply of clean water would improve public health in many developing countries. ——供应洁净水将改善许多发展中国家的公共健康。

### 水供应：Water Supply
- Urban life would be impossible without water supply systems. ——没有供水系统城市生活无从谈起。
- These systems are massive engineering projects. ——这些系统是庞大的工程项目。
- Many professionals are involved in their planning, construction and maintenance. ——众多专业人员参与其规划、建设和维护。
- The supply and distribution of water are major concerns. ——水的供应与调配是重大课题。
- Water is becoming scarce in some countries. ——一些国家水资源日益稀缺。
- Areas that suffer droughts often need to import water. ——干旱地区常需进口水。
- As populations grow, there is more pressure on water supplies. ——人口增长给供水带来更大压力。
- This could lead to a water crisis. ——这可能引发水危机。

### 水与政治：Water and Politics
- The supply of water is also an important political issue. ——供水也是重要政治议题。
- Huge amounts of water are needed for agriculture and industry. ——农业和工业需要大量用水。
- The irrigation of crops accounts for a large proportion of water use. ——灌溉用水占很大比重。
- A water crisis could lead to political conflicts or even wars. ——水危机可能引发政治冲突甚至战争。

### 正方：Argument: water should be free
- Some people believe that water should be free for everyone. ——有人认为水应人人免费。
- Governments should supply water to all homes at no cost. ——政府应免费向所有家庭供水。
- Private companies should not be allowed to profit from this natural resource. ——不应允许私营公司从这一自然资源中牟利。
- Money from taxes can be used to pay for water supply systems. ——可用税款支付供水系统费用。

### 反方：Argument: water should not be free
- If water is free, people take it for granted. ——水免费，人们就不珍惜。
- They do not think about how much water they waste. ——他们不考虑自己浪费了多少水。
- They leave taps running while washing or brushing their teeth. ——洗漱刷牙时水龙头一直开着。
- If we have to pay for water, we will use it more responsibly. ——如果水要花钱，我们会更节约地用。
- Water supply systems are extremely expensive. ——供水系统极其昂贵。
- Investment is needed to maintain and improve them. ——维护和升级都需要投资。
- Private companies may provide a better service than governments. ——私营公司可能比政府服务更好。
- If they provide an efficient service, they will make more money. ——服务高效就能多挣钱。
- They will repair leaks to avoid losing money. ——它们会及时修漏以免亏钱。

### 瓶装水：Bottled Water: Opinions
- Some people carry bottles of water wherever they go. ——有些人走到哪都带瓶装水。
- For example, they take bottles of water to work or to the gym. ——比如带水上班或去健身房。
- They believe that bottled water is healthier than tap water. ——他们认为瓶装水比自来水更健康。
- They also argue that it tastes better. ——他们还说瓶装水口感更好。
- However, other people believe that we should consume less bottled water. ——然而另一些人认为应少喝瓶装水。
- Plastic water bottles add to litter and waste problems. ——塑料水瓶加剧垃圾和浪费问题。
- Companies should not be able to make a profit from water. ——公司不应从水上赚钱。
- It is unethical to make money by selling packaged water. ——靠卖包装水牟利不道德。
- There is no difference in quality between bottled and tap water. ——瓶装水和自来水水质没有差别。

【常用论证角度】健康与生存、经济（成本/投资/利润）、行为激励（收费促节约）、环境（塑料垃圾）、政治冲突、公平（自然资源该不该私有化）。

# 第四部分 观点生成方法：从全书归纳的"想观点公式"

> 本书观点虽按话题罗列，但背后存在高度可复用的生成套路。以下公式全部提炼自书中观点的组织方式；遇到书中没有的全新话题，按公式现场生成即可。

## 4.1 拿到题目后的三步流程
1. **定题型**（Simon 在 family-history 示范中的第一动作）：
   - Opinion（同意与否）/ Discussion（讨论双方）/ Advantages & Disadvantages / 2-part question（两问）/ Problem & Solution。
   - 书中提醒：审题决定写什么——"The task is NOT to write... The task is to explain why..., and then give your opinion"。
2. **定立场**：Simon 常用的是**有条件的中间立场**（先让步再限制），句式如：
   - "To a certain extent censorship is necessary." / "However, immigration needs to be controlled." / "Advertising should be regulated." / "The benefits of mobile phones outweigh the drawbacks. We need to use these phones with care."
3. **生成观点**：用下面的"主体 × 角度"矩阵快速扫出 6~10 个候选观点，挑 2 个最有把握的写主体段。
   - 每个主体段展开方式（书中观点句 → 成文）：观点句（短而明确）→ 解释原因 → 举例子（书中惯用真实例子：伦敦拥堵费、英国NHS、英国缺水管工、世界语、英国"每天五份果蔬"建议等）。

## 4.2 公式一：主体扫描（谁在做/谁该做）
题目中的任何对象，都可以问"各主体怎么看/该做什么"：

- **政府**（书中 "What governments can do" 原文工具箱，13 条，任何题都可用）：
  提供公共服务（医疗/教育）；扶助贫困者；征税；花钱买资源和做宣传；立新法；提高公众意识（气候变化/健康饮食）；影响民众习惯与观点；创造就业；监管企业（如银行）；给学校供资源；保障公民安全福祉；掌管军警。
- **企业**：创造就业（跨国公司在发展中国家建厂）；投资；创新；但也会逐利——迁移到廉价劳动力国家造成失业、剥削员工、过度包装、把营销对准儿童；应生产耐用商品、少用包装。
- **学校**：制定行为守则；与家长紧密合作；聘请心理老师；增加体育课时；菜单禁垃圾食品；把捣乱学生请出课堂。
- **家长**：支持校规、对孩子的行为负责；检查孩子看的内容；不溺爱；花时间陪伴。
- **媒体/电视公司**：报道影响公众（也制造恐慌）；应制作更多积极、有教育意义的节目；儿童节目广告应严管。
- **个人**：更环保（少坐飞机、多坐公交、少买过度包装、回收）；少加班、定期休假；谨慎使用手机；自食其力。

## 4.3 公式二：层面扫描（对谁有影响）
- **个人层面**：健康、金钱、时间、技能、心理（自尊/压力/幸福感）。
  例（书中）：留学者"become more independent"；分班"damage students' self esteem"。
- **家庭层面**：陪伴、代际关系、抚养与赡养负担。
  例："Busy parents have less contact with their children."
- **社会层面**：犯罪、公平、文化多样性、社区感。
  例："Juvenile delinquency is on the increase." / "Immigration also creates cultural diversity."
- **国家层面**：经济、税负、公共服务、国际形象。
  例："This situation will increase the burden on hospitals and taxpayers." / "The Olympic Games are an advertisement for the host nation."
- **全球层面**：环境、和平、贸易、冲突。
  例："Global trade creates more waste and pollution." / "A water crisis could lead to political conflicts or even wars."

## 4.4 公式三：万能角度清单扫描
直接用第二部分的 16 个角度逐个提问："这件事在【金钱/时间/健康/环境/效率/公平/心理/安全/教育/权利/文化/质量/责任/长远短期/个人社会/依赖】上带来什么好处或坏处？"
- 好处和坏处往往**角度对称**：省钱 ↔ 浪费纳税人的钱；安全 ↔ 侵犯自由；效率 ↔ 剥夺基本功（"Their handwriting and spelling may suffer"）；福利减贫 ↔ 福利养懒人。
- 扫出的观点再用"政府/企业/个人"归位，就知道该写"谁应该怎么做"。

## 4.5 公式四：让步 + 限制（Simon 的立场模板）
正反都写完后，落到一个"管起来"的结论上，是全书最高频的收尾方式：
- "Advertising should be regulated." / "There should be age limits for websites and computer games." / "There should be global regulations for salaries and working conditions." / "Governments should impose laws to protect the environment."
- 模板：**承认好处 → 指出风险 → 提出监管/控制/教育措施**。

## 4.6 现场示范一：公共交通该不该免费？
> 题目（自拟新题，书中未覆盖）："Some people think that public transport in cities should be free. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

**第一步定题型**：Opinion 题，需要明确立场；可取 Simon 式中间立场（部分人群免费 + 优先改善服务）。
**第二步主体扫描**：政府（工具箱：征税筹资、提供公共服务、立法、提意识）、公交公司（服务质量和效率）、市民个人（出行习惯、是否珍惜）。
**第三步角度扫描生成观点**（括号内为所用角度与书中同款观点）：
- 正方（公平）："Good healthcare should be available to everyone for free" 的同款逻辑——公共交通是基本服务，免费能帮助低收入者通勤、减少"交通贫困"。
- 正方（环境）："Public transport can help to reduce pollution in cities."——免票能吸引开车的人换乘公交，进一步减排减堵。
- 正方（效率）："Investment in buses and trains will ease traffic congestion."——更多人坐公交，道路空间利用更高效。
- 反方（金钱/税负）："The benefits system is a burden on taxpayers." 同款——免费意味着政府失去票款收入，全靠税收补贴，纳税人负担加重。
- 反方（行为激励）："If water is free, people take it for granted."——免费乘车会被滥用：短途也坐、占座闲逛，车厢更拥挤（"People feel like sardines in a can"），体验反而变差。
- 反方（质量优先）："Public transport needs to be reliable and efficient."——钱应先花在提升准点率和舒适度上，服务差才是人们不敢坐的根因（"Public transport is often slow and unreliable"）。
**第四步落立场**（让步+限制）："免费公交有助于公平和环保，to a certain extent 应该做；但更合理的做法是对学生、老人等群体免费，同时把资金优先用于改善服务。"——这正是 "Governments should only censor false information..." 一类"有条件的监管"句式。

## 4.7 现场示范二：太空探索是不是浪费钱？
> 题目（自拟新题，书中未覆盖）："Some people think the money spent on space exploration is a waste of resources. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

**第一步定题型**：Opinion 题。
**第二步主体扫描**：政府（花钱优先级）、科学家/航天机构（技术与教育）、国际社会（合作分摊）。
**第三步角度扫描生成观点**（括号内为所用角度与书中同款观点）：
- 反方（机会成本）："Too much money is spent on weapons and military technology. This money could be spent on schools, hospitals and other public services." 同款——在"Food, drinking water and basic medicines are in short supply"的问题还没解决时，太空巨款应先投向民生。
- 反方（风险）："Opponents of nuclear power worry about the safety..." 同款——太空项目投入巨大、失败率高，等于拿纳税人的钱冒险。
- 正方（科技外溢）："Internet, fax and mobile phone technologies have revolutionised working life." 同款——航天项目催生的材料、通信、医疗技术最终惠及普通人。
- 正方（长远）："Fossil fuels like oil and gas are running out."——地球资源有限，太空探索是人类长远的"备用方案"，属于 "investing in long-term projects"。
- 正方（国际合作）："A global economy means free trade between countries. This can strengthen political relationships." 同款——多国联合空间任务能促进国家间的和平与信任。
- 正方（教育/激励）："Zoos are educational, interesting and fun." 同款——太空探索激励年轻人学习科学，造就下一代工程师和科学家。
**第四步落立场**（让步+限制）："太空探索不应被完全否定，但规模应受控制、成本应由国际合作分摊，并优先保障民生开支。"——同 "Governments should limit the production of nuclear weapons" 的句式逻辑。
